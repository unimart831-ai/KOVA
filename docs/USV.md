# Kova — USV Grant Application (Mastercard Foundation)

**April 2026**

---

### What problem does your solution address?

44 million African small businesses depend on social media as their primary way to find customers, but lack the time, expertise, and affordable tools to do it effectively — resulting in inconsistent presence, missed buyer inquiries, and lost revenue.

---

### Who are the main people affected by this problem?

African SME owners — retailers, restaurants, salons, consultants, creatives, e-commerce sellers, and service providers — who know social media is their primary customer acquisition channel but lack the time (3–5 hours/day required), money (a social media manager costs KES 30,000–50,000/month), and expertise to do it consistently. They juggle operations, sales, and delivery while their online presence stalls, costing them customers and growth.

---

### What is your proposed solution?

An AI-powered Business Intelligence Operating System that gives African small businesses an autonomous digital marketing team — 6 AI agents that research trends, create platform-native content, publish across 9 social platforms, engage audiences, capture leads, and nurture them via email — starting at $2/month with M-Pesa payments.
