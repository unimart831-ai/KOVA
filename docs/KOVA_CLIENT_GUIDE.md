## How It Works — 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Tell Kova About Your Business (5 minutes, once)

When you sign up, you complete a quick 3-step setup:

1. **Brand Basics** — Your company name, website, industry, content language (11 languages including Swahili, Sheng, Pidgin), and key products/services you sell
2. **Your Voice** — Select tone attributes from a visual grid (confident, witty, bold, etc.), describe your brand voice in detail, paste real examples of how you sound, set your target audience, pick content pillars (with industry-smart suggestions), and define brand guardrails (topics and words the AI must avoid)
3. **Your Goals & Preferences** — Choose your goals, set posting frequency (enforced by AI), toggle auto-approve and auto-engage (AI replies to positive comments)

That's it. In 5 minutes, Kova captures your complete brand DNA — tone, language, products, audience, and restrictions. Your AI team knows your brand better than a freelancer would after 3 meetings.

### Step 2: Connect Your Social Accounts (2 minutes)

Connect the platforms where your customers are:

- **Facebook** — Pages and business accounts
- **Instagram** — Professional accounts
- **X (Twitter)** — Business or personal
- **LinkedIn** — Personal and company pages
- **TikTok** — Business accounts
- **YouTube** — Channels
- **Pinterest** — Business accounts
- **Threads** — Professional accounts
- **Bluesky** — Accounts

Connect one account or all nine. Kova adapts content for each platform automatically.

### Step 3: Drop Ideas, Approve Content (5 minutes daily)

This is your daily workflow:

1. **Morning** — Open Kova. Read your Daily Brief (what happened yesterday, what's trending today, what content is ready)
2. **Review** — Look at AI-generated posts. Edit if you want. Approve the ones you like. Reject what doesn't fit.
3. **Done** — Go run your business. Kova publishes on schedule, responds to comments, and tracks everything.

That's 5 minutes. Your AI team handles the other 23 hours and 55 minutes.

## Meet Your AI Team

When you join Kova, you don't get a tool. You get a team of 6 AI agents, each with a specific job:

### 🔍 The Researcher

**"What should we talk about today?"**

Your Research Agent scans trends in your industry, monitors what's buzzing in your niche, and finds content opportunities — before they become yesterday's news.

- Discovers 5-8 trending topics relevant to YOUR business every day
- Factors in your actual products/services — trends are relevant to what you sell, not just your industry
- Researches in your content language — finds trends in the markets where you operate
- Creates opportunity briefs: "Here's a hot topic, here's why it matters to you, here's the angle to take"
- Identifies seasonal moments and emerging conversations
- Feeds intelligence to the rest of the team

*You never have to wonder "what should I post?" again.*

### ✍️ The Content Creator

**"Let me write that for you."**

Your Creator Agent generates platform-native content in your brand voice — not generic AI text that sounds like everyone else.

- Takes your raw ideas ("share tips about saving for school fees") and turns them into polished posts
- Uses your tone attributes (confident? witty? bold?) to match HOW you speak, not just what you say
- Writes in your chosen language — Swahili, Sheng, Pidgin, French mixes, or English — using native patterns, not translations
- References your actual products and services naturally in content
- Respects your brand guardrails — topics, words, and approaches you said to avoid are NEVER used
- Writes differently for each platform (Facebook caption vs. Twitter hook vs. LinkedIn article)
- Uses proven frameworks: Hook → Value → Call-to-Action
- Learns from your best-performing posts and biases toward what works
- Generates AI images alongside your content (Growth plan and above)

*You approve. It publishes. That simple.*

### 📊 The Analyst

**"Here's what's working and what's not."**

Your Analyst Agent tracks every metric and finds patterns that matter.

- Tracks impressions, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, and clicks for every post
- Identifies your winning Content DNA — which formats, tones, hooks, and topics drive the most engagement
- Predicts how well a post will perform before you publish it (engagement score 0-100)
- Feeds performance data back to the Creator so your next post is better

*Over time, your content gets smarter. Automatically.*

### ⏰ The Scheduler

**"I know when your audience is online."**

Your Adapt Agent analyzes 30 days of your posting history to find the exact hours and days when your audience is most active.

- Identifies your best hours per platform (e.g., "Your LinkedIn audience peaks at 12pm and 6pm")
- Identifies your best days (e.g., "Tuesday and Thursday get 2x more engagement")
- Schedules in YOUR timezone — if you're in Nairobi, 9 AM means 9 AM East Africa Time, not server time
- Respects your posting frequency budget — if you set 7/week, it stops at 7. No over-posting
- Automatically schedules posts at optimal times
- Adjusts as your audience behavior changes

*No more guessing when to post. No more posting at the wrong time for your audience.*

### 💬 The Engagement Manager

**"I'll handle the comments and DMs."**

Your Engage Agent monitors every comment, mention, and message across all your platforms — and responds intelligently.

- Fetches comments and mentions every 30 minutes
- Analyzes sentiment: Is this person happy? Frustrated? Asking a question?
- Generates contextual replies in your brand voice
- Auto-responds to positive comments (when you enable it)
- Flags urgent items: complaints, purchase intent, influencer mentions
- Detects your **superfans** — people who engage with you repeatedly

*Your audience feels heard. Even when you're asleep.*

### 🧠 The Chief Strategist

**"Here's the big picture."**

Your Strategist is the brain of the operation. It reads data from ALL other agents and makes coordinated decisions.

- Compiles your **Daily Brief** every morning: what happened, what's trending, what needs your attention
- Decides what content to create proactively (based on trends + performance + engagement data)
- Recommends strategic moves: "Your engagement dipped — here are 3 things to try"
- Monitors competitor gaps and finds opportunities they're missing
- Creates content seeds automatically — your pipeline never runs dry

*This is the agent that makes Kova truly autonomous. It doesn't just execute — it thinks.*

## Common Questions

### "Will the AI content sound like me?"

**Yes.** During onboarding, you describe your brand voice (friendly? professional? playful?). Kova uses this + your approved posts to learn your exact style. The more you use it, the better it sounds. After 2-3 weeks, most users say they can't tell the difference between AI posts and their own.

### "What if I don't like what the AI creates?"

**You're always in control.** Every post starts as a draft. You review it, edit it, approve it, or reject it. Nothing publishes without your approval (unless you specifically turn on Auto-Approve on the Biashara plan).

### "Is my data safe?"

**Yes.** Your social media credentials are secured with OAuth (industry standard — we never see your password). Your content and analytics are private to your account. We don't share your data with other users or third parties.

### "What happens when my trial ends?"

You'll get an email reminder before your 14-day trial ends. If you choose a plan, you continue seamlessly. If you don't subscribe, your account stays active on the Starter plan features. Your content, analytics, and connections are never deleted.

**Anytime.** Upgrade or downgrade from your billing dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

**M-Pesa** — For Kenyan users. Enter your phone number, confirm the STK push on your phone. No card needed.

**Stripe** — For international users. Visa, Mastercard, and other cards accepted.

### "What platforms are supported?"
