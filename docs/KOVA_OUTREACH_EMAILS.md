## Email Best Practices

### Before Sending

- [ ] Personalize — reference something specific about the recipient (their work, company, recent news)
- [ ] Subject line is specific and value-first (not "Partnership Opportunity")
- [ ] Under 200 words for cold outreach (longer only for formal grant/corporate proposals)
- [ ] Clear ask — one specific request (meeting, demo, intro), not multiple
- [ ] Proofread — one typo can kill credibility

### General Rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with THEIR problem or interest | Lead with "I am writing to..." |
| Be specific about what you want | Say "let's explore synergies" |
| Include proof (live product, metrics) | Make vague claims |
| Keep cold emails under 200 words | Write 500-word first emails |
| Follow up 2-3 times, then stop | Send 7 follow-ups |
| Personalize every email | Blast the same template to 100 people |
| Make it easy to say yes ("15 minutes, any day this week") | Leave the next step ambiguous |
