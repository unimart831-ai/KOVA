## 1. PROFILE PHOTO & BANNER

### Profile Photo Guidelines
- **Style**: Professional but approachable — you're a tech founder, not a banker
- **Background**: Clean, solid color or blurred tech/city environment (Nairobi skyline works great)
- **Expression**: Confident smile, direct eye contact
- **Framing**: Head and shoulders, face takes up ~60% of the frame
- **Lighting**: Natural light, no harsh shadows
- **Clothing**: Smart casual — collared shirt or clean polo, no suit unless that's your style
- **Resolution**: Minimum 400x400px, upload at 800x800px for sharpness
- **Pro tip**: Take the photo in landscape, then crop to square — gives you the best framing options

### Banner Image (1584 x 396px)
Create a custom banner that communicates your mission at a glance. Here's what it should include:

**Option A — Brand-Forward Banner:**
- Left side: Kova Agent logo
- Center text: **"Every African business deserves an AI team."**
- Right side: Subtle visual of connected platforms (social media icons in a loop)
- Color scheme: Match Kova's brand colors
- Bottom-right corner: `kovaagent.com` (or your production URL)

**Option B — Personal Mission Banner:**
- Text: **"Building Autonomous Social Intelligence for Africa"**
- Subtext: **"Founder & CEO @ Kova Agent"**
- Background: Abstract tech pattern or Nairobi cityscape with dark overlay
- Clean, minimal — let the words carry weight

**Tools to create it**: Canva (free), Figma, or commission from a designer on Fiverr ($15-30)

## 8. PROFILE SETTINGS & OPTIMIZATIONS

### Must-Do Settings

1. **Custom URL**: `linkedin.com/in/[yourname]` — Go to Profile → Edit public profile → Customize URL
2. **Open to Work / Hiring**: Turn OFF "Open to Work." Turn ON "Providing Services" if applicable
3. **Profile visibility**: Set to "Public" — you want everyone finding you
4. **Email visibility**: Show to connections only (not public)
5. **Connection requests**: Allow anyone (don't restrict — you're building a network)
6. **Newsletter**: Consider starting a LinkedIn Newsletter for long-form content (e.g., "The Autonomy Loop — Weekly insights on AI, Africa, and building in public")

### SEO Keywords to Weave Throughout Profile
LinkedIn has a search algorithm. These keywords should appear naturally across your headline, about, experience, and skills:

- AI agents
- Social media management
- African tech / Africa tech
- SaaS founder
- Django developer
- Autonomous AI
- Social media intelligence
- Content automation
- M-Pesa / mobile payments
- Startup founder Kenya
- AI for business
- Social media marketing

### Engagement Settings
- **Notifications**: Turn on for all post engagement (comments, shares)
- **Reply to every comment** on your posts for the first 30 days minimum
- **Comment on 5-10 posts daily** from people in your target network
- Set aside 15-20 minutes morning and evening for LinkedIn engagement
