# KOVA Growth Partners Program

> **Earn while you grow the future of AI-powered social media.**

---

## Overview

The Kova Growth Partners Program is a performance-based partnership that rewards individuals and businesses for referring new paying clients to Kova Agent. Instead of traditional equity or salary arrangements, partners earn recurring commissions, milestone bonuses, and — at scale — a share of company profits.

This is not affiliate marketing. This is a growth partnership. You bring clients. They succeed. You earn — month after month, for up to 24 months per client.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Apply & Get Approved

Submit your application through the Growth Partners page. We review your profile, audience, and alignment with Kova's mission. Approved partners receive:

- A **unique referral code** (e.g., `KOVA-JAMES-2025`)
- A **personalized referral link** (e.g., `app.kovaagent.com/?ref=KOVA-JAMES-2025`)
- Access to the **Partner Dashboard** with real-time tracking

### 2. Share Your Link

When someone signs up using your referral link or enters your code during registration, they're permanently tagged as your referral. You can share via:

- Social media posts
- Direct messages
- Email newsletters
- Blog articles
- YouTube/TikTok reviews
- In-person recommendations

### 3. Earn When They Pay

You earn commission on every payment your referred clients make — not just the first one. Commission is calculated on the **net subscription revenue** (after payment processing fees) and continues for **24 months** per client.

## Layer 1: Recurring Commission

Your commission rate increases as you bring more active clients.

| Active Clients | Commission Rate | Example Monthly Earning (Growth plan) |
|---------------|----------------|---------------------------------------|
| 1–25          | **15%**        | 25 clients × KES 999 × 15% = **KES 3,746/mo** |
| 26–75         | **20%**        | 75 clients × KES 999 × 20% = **KES 14,985/mo** |
| 76–150        | **25%**        | 150 clients × KES 999 × 25% = **KES 37,463/mo** |
| 150+          | **30%**        | 200 clients × KES 999 × 30% = **KES 59,940/mo** |

### Key Rules

- **"Active" means paid for 2+ consecutive months.** A client who signs up but cancels after one month doesn't count toward your tier.
- **Commission lasts 24 months per client.** After 24 months, that client's payments no longer generate commission for you (but they still count toward your tier).
- **Your tier is recalculated monthly** based on current active client count.
- **Mix of plans counts.** If you refer 10 Starter (KES 299), 30 Growth (KES 999), and 5 Pro (KES 1,999) clients, commission is calculated on each plan's revenue individually.

## Layer 3: Profit Share (Elite Partners)

For partners who prove they can drive sustained, high-quality growth, Kova offers a share of quarterly net profits.

| Requirement | Profit Share | Additional Benefit |
|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **100+ active clients** (avg 6-month retention) | **1%** of quarterly net profit | Annual partnership contract |
| **200+ active clients** (avg 6-month retention) | **2%** of quarterly net profit | Priority support line |
| **500+ active clients** (avg 6-month retention) | **3%** of quarterly net profit | Advisory Board seat |

### Rules

- Profit share is **in addition to** your recurring commission and milestone bonuses.
- "Net profit" = total revenue minus all operating costs, payment fees, and taxes.
- Reviewed **quarterly**. If your active client count drops below the threshold for **2 consecutive quarters**, profit share pauses until you're back above.
- Maximum of **10 profit share partners** at any time. Total pool capped at **15% of net profit**.
- Requires a signed annual partnership agreement with quarterly review.
- The 6-month average retention requirement prevents gaming — you can't just sign up 100 people who all churn.

## Anti-Gaming Rules

We've designed the program to reward genuine growth, not manipulation.

1. **2-Month Activation Period**: A referred client must make at least 2 consecutive monthly payments before counting as "active" and generating commission.
2. **Unique Referral Codes Only**: No retroactive attribution. The referral code must be used at signup.
3. **No Self-Referral**: You cannot refer yourself or accounts you control.
4. **Verified Business**: Referred clients must be legitimate businesses or individuals with genuine social media management needs.
5. **Retention Requirement for Profit Share**: Minimum 6-month average client retention to qualify for Layer 3.
6. **24-Month Cap**: Commission on any single client stops after 24 months from their first payment.
7. **Fraud = Termination**: Fake accounts, incentivized signups (paying people to sign up), or misrepresentation results in immediate removal and forfeiture of pending payouts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to be a Kova customer to be a partner?**
A: No, but we do give you a free Pro account so you can speak authentically about the product.

**Q: What if my referred client upgrades their plan?**
A: Your commission automatically adjusts to the new plan amount. If they upgrade from Starter (KES 299) to Growth (KES 999), your next month's commission is based on KES 999.

**Q: What if a client downgrades or cancels?**
A: Commission adjusts to the new plan. If they cancel, commission stops for that client — but they still count toward your milestone progress.

**Q: Can I promote Kova through paid ads?**
A: Yes, with restrictions. You cannot bid on "Kova" or "Kova Agent" brand keywords. You cannot make misleading claims about the product.

**Q: How long after signup do I start earning?**
A: After your referred client completes 2 consecutive monthly payments (the activation period). Your first commission for that client is paid on the next monthly payout cycle.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many clients I can refer?**
A: No limit. The more you refer, the higher your tier and the more you earn per client.

**Q: Can I be a partner and an agency client?**
A: Absolutely. Many of our best partners use Kova for their own clients AND refer other businesses.

**Q: What happens after the 24-month commission period ends for a client?**
A: Commission for that specific client stops, but they still count toward your active client total for tier purposes as long as they remain subscribed.
