# KOVA BIOS — Seed Funding Proposal

---

## Problem

As small business owners in Kenya try to find customers on social media, they cannot
afford the time, expertise, or tools to do it consistently. This problem affects
**7.4 million small businesses in Kenya** (44 million across Africa). Fewer than 8%
of African SMEs maintain a consistent social media presence, and hiring a social media
manager costs KES 30,000–50,000 per month — more than most small businesses earn in
monthly profit.

---

## Solution

Our solution is **Kova BIOS — a Business Intelligence Operating System for African
small businesses**. It helps small business owners grow their customer base through
social media more affordably, consistently, and intelligently — with 6 AI agents
handling content creation, publishing, engagement, and email marketing automatically,
starting at **KES 299 per month via M-Pesa**.

---

## Scalability / Innovation

Other tools like Buffer, Hootsuite, and Sprout Social offer social media scheduling,
but their offerings are too expensive ($25–$249/month) and culturally disconnected
from African businesses. Our offering will be more accessible because we built for
Africa from day one: KES pricing, M-Pesa payments, 6 AI agents running 24/7, and a
complete loop from content creation to lead capture to email marketing — in one
platform, at African prices. So, we could grow to reach **all 44 million MSMEs across
Africa**.

## Social & Environmental Impact

Wider benefits of our solution's use will be **stronger economic growth for African
small businesses and more equal access to professional digital marketing tools**.
We'll measure these with:

- **KPI 1:** Number of MSMEs posting consistently (3+ times per week) vs. national

- **KPI 3:** Number of customer leads captured per user per month through Kova's
  conversion forms
- **KPI 4:** Number of women-owned businesses onboarded and actively using Kova

## Market Validation / Risk Mitigation

The biggest assumptions behind our venture model have been validated with the
following 3+ prototyping tests:

1. **Small business owners will pay KES 999/month for AI social media management.**
   We demonstrated Kova to 12 SME owners across Nairobi — salons, restaurants, and
   retailers. 9 out of 12 said they would pay KES 999/month, citing time saved and
   the cost vs. hiring a social media manager (KES 30,000+/month). 3 owners requested
   immediate beta access.

2. **AI can create high-quality, culturally relevant content for Kenyan businesses.**
   We ran 14-day content tests on 3 real business social accounts. AI-generated posts
   achieved an average engagement rate of 4.2% compared to the owners' previous 1.1%
   — a 3.8× improvement — with all three owners confirming the content "sounds like
   us."

3. **M-Pesa works reliably as a recurring SaaS payment method without a credit card.**
   We built and tested the full M-Pesa STK Push integration using Safaricom's Daraja
   API. All 5 test users successfully completed subscription payments via M-Pesa, with
   auto-renewal working correctly — proving the billing model is fully viable for the
   Kenyan market.
