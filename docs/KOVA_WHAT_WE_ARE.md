## Sales call script (15 minutes)

### Open (2 min)
"Thanks for jumping on. Before I show anything — what's eating most of your time with social media right now: creating content, posting consistently, replying to people, or knowing what's actually working?"

*Listen. Mirror their pain back in their words.*

### Frame (1 min)
"That's exactly what Kova is built for. We're not another tool where you stare at a blank screen. You get an AI team — six agents — that prepares your week while you sleep. You spend about five minutes approving what goes live."

### Show (5 min) — in this order
1. **Daily Brief** — "This is your morning. Pipeline, trends, what needs attention."
2. **Studio** — "Drafts land here. Edit, approve, reject. One idea becomes native posts per platform."
3. **Queue / Calendar** — "Approved posts schedule and publish automatically."
4. **Optional by pain:** Engage (inbox), Products (Snap to Sell), Leads, Email, Bookings.

### Proof / differentiation (3 min)
- "Post number fifty should outperform post number one — Content DNA learns what works for *your* audience."
- "One idea → different angle on Instagram vs LinkedIn vs X — not copy-paste."
- "Priced for African SMBs. M-Pesa. KES plans. Not $99/month US tools."

### Close (2 min)
"Best next step is a 14-day free trial. Connect one platform, run through onboarding — you'll have draft posts and a welcome brief before you finish setup. Want to start today or should I send a link?"

## What we ARE / are NOT

**We ARE:**
- An AI marketing team with a 5-minute daily approval habit
- Platform-native content (not reformatted — rethought per network)
- Africa-first: KES pricing, M-Pesa, local context (calendar, memes, trends)
- A path from posts → leads → bookings → revenue attribution

**We are NOT:**
- A blank-screen scheduler (Buffer, Later)
- A generic AI writer (ChatGPT, Jasper)
- A enterprise dashboard (Hootsuite at $99+)
- "Set and forget" by default — we're honest about human oversight

## Voice & tone

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Clear, confident, warm | Corporate jargon, "leverage synergies" |
| Specific numbers (5 min, 6 agents, KES 299) | Vague "AI-powered solution" |
| African context when relevant | Silicon Valley cosplay |
| Outcome-first ("results from social") | Feature dumps ("we have 47 modules") |
| Honest about approval step | Overpromise full autonomy |

**Words we like:** team, approve, results, running start, platform-native, learn, loop, brief, African businesses  
**Words we avoid:** disrupt, revolutionary (unless earned), magic (unless Magic Fill product name), guaranteed viral
