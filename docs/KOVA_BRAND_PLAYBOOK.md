## 1. BRAND IDENTITY FOUNDATION

### Who Kova Agent Is

**One-liner**: The AI team every African business deserves.

**Category**: Business Intelligence Operating System (we created this category — own it)

**What we are NOT**:
- Not a scheduling tool (Buffer, Later)
- Not a social dashboard (Hootsuite, Sprout Social)
- Not a content writer (Jasper, Copy.ai)
- Not a virtual assistant

**What we ARE**:
- 6 AI agents working 24/7 as your social media department
- A system that researches, creates, predicts, schedules, publishes, engages, and learns
- The first platform that gets smarter with every post (Content DNA)
- Africa's first, built in Nairobi, priced for African businesses

### Brand Personality (The Human Behind the Brand)

If Kova Agent were a person, they'd be:

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Age** | Mid-20s — young enough to understand the culture, experienced enough to deliver |
| **Energy** | High-energy but not chaotic. Focused intensity. Building-in-public energy |
| **Vibe** | The smartest friend in your WhatsApp group who actually ships things |
| **Humor** | Sharp, witty, observational — never corny. Laughs at the hustle, never at the hustler |
| **Confidence** | Earned, not arrogant. Shows receipts. "We built this" not "We're the best" |
| **Values** | Entrepreneurship, accessibility, African innovation, radical honesty, action > talk |
| **Speaking style** | Clear, direct, no fluff. Breaks complex AI concepts into relatable language |

### Core Beliefs (These Drive Every Post)

1. **Every African business deserves an AI team** — not just the funded ones
2. **Your time is your most valuable asset** — stop trading it for repetitive tasks
3. **AI should work FOR you, not replace you** — you're the strategist, AI is the team
4. **Price should never be the barrier** — KES 299/month proves technology can be accessible
5. **The best tool is the one that gets smarter** — not the one with the most features
6. **Show, don't tell** — we ship publicly, share metrics, and prove claims with evidence
7. **Africa doesn't need adapted tools, Africa needs built-for-Africa tools**

## 2. BRAND VOICE DEFINITION

### Voice Attributes

| Dimension | Kova's Position | Never |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| **Tone** | Confident & approachable | Arrogant, salesy, desperate |
| **Language** | Clear, conversational, occasionally bold | Jargon-heavy, corporate-speak, generic |
| **Humor** | Sharp, observational, relatable | Dadj jokes, forced memes, sarcasm that punches down |
| **Emotion** | Passionate about the mission, empathetic about pain points | Cold, robotic, preachy |
| **Complexity** | Simple enough for non-tech people, smart enough for developers | Dumbed-down or unnecessarily academic |
| **Cultural** | Proudly African, naturally multilingual (English/Swahili/Sheng) | Performatively woke, stereotypical, or culturally detached |
| **CTA style** | Invitational ("Try this"), challenger ("Are you still doing it the old way?") | Pushy ("Buy now!"), manipulative scarcity |

### Voice Examples — What Kova Sounds Like

**On product capability:**
> "Your social media manager charges $500/month, works 8 hours, takes weekends off, and still asks what to post. Kova's 6 AI agents work 24/7, learn your brand's DNA, and cost less than lunch in Nairobi."

**On a win:**
> "First Instagram reel published via Kova yesterday. Zero human intervention: Research Agent found the trending topic → Create Agent wrote the script → Adapt Agent picked 7:14 PM (peak engagement) → Published automatically. The loop works."

**On a pain point:**
> "You didn't start your business to spend 3 hours a day writing captions. You started it because you saw a problem worth solving. Let the AI handle the captions. You handle the vision."

**On competition:**
> "Hootsuite gives you a dashboard. Buffer gives you a timer. Jasper gives you a writer. Kova gives you all six — Research, Create, Predict, Schedule, Engage, Learn — in one autonomous loop. For $2/month."

**On Africa:**
> "When we say 'built for Africa,' we don't mean 'adapted for Africa.' We mean: built in Nairobi, tested with Kenyan businesses, priced in KES, paid via M-Pesa, optimized for mobile, and designed for the entrepreneur running 5 things at once."

**Casual / community moments:**
> "Monday morning. Your competitors are staring at a blank content calendar. Your Kova Daily Brief already has 5 posts ready to approve, 3 trending topics in your niche, and optimal publishing times calculated. Sip your chai, approve, and move on. 🍵"

### Language Rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Use numbers for impact | "6 agents, 9 platforms, $2/month" not "multiple agents, many platforms, affordable" |
| Lead with the insight, not the feature | "After 30 days, Kova knows your audience better than you do" not "We have a Content DNA feature" |
| Concrete > abstract | "Published 147 posts last week across 9 platforms" not "We help businesses scale their social presence" |
| Short sentences for punchy claims | "Not a scheduler. Not a dashboard. An AI team." |
| Active voice always | "Kova researches trends and creates content" not "Trends are researched and content is created" |
| Use Swahili/Sheng naturally, never forced | "Kazi mode 🔥" is good. "Karibu to our platform, habari!" is cringe |
| Hashtags: purposeful, not decorative | Max 3-5 per post. Always include #AfricaTech on relevant content |
| Emojis: strategic accent, not decoration | Use 1-3 max per post. 🔥 💡 🚀 ✅ are on-brand. 🥰 😍 💕 are not |

### Banned Words & Phrases

Never use these — they're generic, overused, or off-brand:

| Banned | Use Instead |
|--------|-------------|
| "Revolutionize" | "Replace" or "rebuild" |
| "Cutting-edge" | Be specific about what's new |
| "Leverage" | "Use" |
| "Synergy" | Just... no |
| "World-class" | Show it, don't claim it |
| "Game-changer" | Describe the actual change |
| "Empower" | "Give you" or "unlock" |
| "Seamless" | Describe the actual experience |
| "Unlock your potential" | "Stop wasting time on [specific thing]" |
| "We're excited to announce" | Just announce it. "New:" or "Shipped:" |
| "Stay tuned" | Give a specific date or action |

## 4. CONTENT STRATEGY & PILLARS

### The 5 Content Pillars

Every post Kova publishes should map to one of these pillars:

#### Pillar 1 — PRODUCT (30% of content)
**Goal**: Show what Kova does and drive signups

- Feature demos and walkthroughs
- New feature announcements ("Shipped:")
- Side-by-side comparisons (Kova vs manual, Kova vs competitors)
- Screen recordings of the AI agents working
- "How it works" explainers
- Pricing breakdowns and value propositions

**Key framing**: Always show the OUTCOME, not just the feature. Not "We added Content DNA" but "Kova now learns which of your posts get the most engagement and creates more like them."

#### Pillar 2 — BUILD IN PUBLIC (25% of content)
**Goal**: Build trust, authenticity, and founder community

- What we shipped this week
- Technical decisions and trade-offs
- Bug stories and lessons learned
- Metrics and milestones (users, posts published, agent accuracy)
- The real startup journey — wins AND struggles
- Stack and architecture insights

**Key framing**: Honest, specific, educational. Never brag without receipts. "We hit 1,000 posts published this week" with actual numbers.

#### Pillar 3 — SOCIAL MEDIA INTELLIGENCE (20% of content)
**Goal**: Position Kova as the authority on social media strategy

- Platform algorithm insights
- Content strategy tips (not generic — data-backed from Content DNA)
- "Most businesses do X wrong. Here's why."
- Trend analysis and predictions
- Format/timing/engagement best practices
- Myth-busting (e.g., "Posting every day doesn't help if your content sucks")

**Key framing**: Teach people things that make them go "I didn't know that." Become their go-to source for social media intelligence.

#### Pillar 4 — AFRICA TECH & MISSION (15% of content)
**Goal**: Rally the community, attract investors, inspire builders

- "Built for Africa" stories
- African entrepreneurship spotlights
- The tech-for-Africa opportunity and why it matters now
- M-Pesa and mobile-first innovation
- Hot takes on the African tech ecosystem
- "44 million MSMEs" narrative — humanize the number

**Key framing**: Proud but not preachy. Celebrating African innovation without being performative about it.

#### Pillar 5 — COMMUNITY & ENGAGEMENT (10% of content)

Platforms that solve part of the same problem but differently. They overlap with Kova on one or two capabilities.

**Tier 3 — Aspirational / Category Leaders (1)**
The big player in the broader space. You're not competing directly today, but their moves shape market expectations.

## 14. CONTENT SEEDING GUIDE

> Content Seeds are the raw ideas you feed into Kova's Create Agent. A seed becomes
> multiple platform-specific posts. The better your seeds, the better the AI output.

### Anatomy of a Good Seed

| Field | Purpose | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Idea** (required) | The core topic, angle, or content direction | "Compare Kova's pricing ($2/mo) to a freelance social media manager ($500/mo) — break down what each gives you" |
| **Notes** (optional) | Extra context/instructions the AI uses | "Use actual KES numbers. Mention M-Pesa. Make it a carousel for IG and a thread for X. Tone: confident, not arrogant." |
| **Target Platforms** (optional) | Restrict to specific platforms | `["instagram", "twitter", "linkedin"]` — if empty, generates for ALL connected platforms |

### Seed Strategy — What to Submit and When

**Seed Cadence:**
- Submit **3-5 seeds per week** to keep the content pipeline full
- Front-load seeds early in the week (Monday/Tuesday) so the AI has time to generate and you have time to review
- Save "evergreen" seeds for slow news weeks
- Submit "reactive" seeds immediately when something timely happens (competitor news, algorithm change, trending topic)

**Seed Types for Different Pillars:**

| Pillar | Seed Example | Notes Field |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Product** | "Show the Daily Brief feature — what a user sees when they wake up" | "Screen recording style description. Focus on time saved." |
| **Build in Public** | "Week 3 metrics: posts published, engagement rate, follower growth" | "Use real numbers. Be honest about what's not working too." |
| **Intelligence** | "Instagram Reels algorithm changes in 2026 — what businesses should do differently" | "Data-backed. Don't be generic. Reference specific changes." |
| **Mission** | "Why we built Kova in Nairobi instead of moving to Silicon Valley" | "Personal founder story angle. Mention the ecosystem, the talent, the market." |
| **Community** | "What's the one social media task you hate most?" | "Keep it simple. Engagement-focused. Good for all platforms." |

### Using Target Platforms Strategically

| Scenario | Target Platforms | Why |
|----------|-----------------|-----|
| Technical deep dive | `["twitter", "linkedin"]` | Too technical for TikTok/IG |
| Visual product demo | `["instagram", "tiktok", "pinterest"]` | Visual-first platforms |
| Casual hot take | `["twitter", "threads", "bluesky"]` | Text-first, conversational platforms |
| Long-form educational | `["linkedin", "facebook", "youtube"]` | Platforms that reward depth |
| Everything (default) | `[]` (empty) | Let the Adapt Agent optimize per platform |

### Batch Strategy and AI Generation

The `batch_strategy` field is AI-generated — the Create Agent writes a content strategy for each seed before generating posts. This includes:
- Angle and hook approach per platform
- Content framework to use (Hook→Value→CTA, PAS, Story→Lesson, etc.)
- Visual strategy recommendation per platform
- Predicted engagement drivers

> You don't fill in `batch_strategy` — the AI does. But reviewing it after generation helps you understand the AI's reasoning and give better seeds next time.

### 20.3 Lead Inbox

**Where:** Sidebar → Conversion → Lead Inbox → `/leads/`

The Lead Inbox is Kova's built-in mini-CRM. Leads are auto-created from form submissions — no manual data entry.

#### How Leads Arrive

```
Visitor fills Kova Form → Django signal fires → Lead auto-created
                                                  → Priority auto-scored
                                                  → Activity logged
```

The signal (`apps/leads/signals.py`) fires on every `FormSubmission` save and:
- Creates a Lead (or updates if a lead with that email already exists)

- Logs a "form_submitted" activity on the lead's timeline

| **Contacted** | You reached out | After first DM, email, or call |

| **Converted** | Deal closed | They signed up, paid, or became a client |
| **Lost** | Didn't work out | Went cold, chose competitor, bad fit |

Move leads through statuses on the lead detail page. Each status change is logged on the activity timeline.

#### Priority System

Priority auto-calculates based on contact info quality:
- **High** — Has email AND phone, or came through a form submission
- **Medium** — Has email but no phone
- **Low** — Missing key contact information

#### Working with Leads — Kova's Own Workflow

2. **Triage:** Open each new lead → review source and contact info
3. **Tag:** Add relevant tags:
   - `trial-interest` — Came through trial CTA
   - `demo-request` — Used contact form
   - `newsletter` — Signed up for updates only
   - `enterprise` — Mentioned company or team needs
   - `nairobi` / `lagos` / `cape-town` — Geographic tags
4. **Contact:** Move to "Contacted" → add note with what you sent
5. **Qualify:** If they respond → move to "Qualified" → add note
6. **Convert or Close:** Move to "Converted" (yay!) or "Lost" (learn from it)

#### Campaign Metrics

After a campaign is sent, the detail page (`/emails/campaigns/<id>/`) shows:

| Metric | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Total Sent | How many emails were dispatched |
| Opened (%) | How many recipients opened the email |
| Clicked (%) | How many clicked a link inside |
| Bounced | Delivery failures (bad email addresses) |
| Unsubscribed | People who opted out |

Goals:
- **Open rate:** 25%+ (industry average is ~20%)
- **Click rate:** 3%+ (industry average is ~2.5%)
- **Bounce rate:** <2% (keep your list clean)

#### Subscriber Engagement Score

Every subscriber has an `engagement_score` (0-100):
- **70-100** (Green) — Highly engaged, opens and clicks regularly
- **40-69** (Amber) — Cooling off, might need re-engagement
- **0-39** (Red) — At risk, consider a win-back campaign or removal

Bounce handling: 3+ bounces auto-marks subscriber as "Bounced" (stops receiving emails).

#### A/B Testing Campaigns

1. Create Campaign A (the original)
2. Create Campaign B → set `variant_of` to Campaign A, `variant_label` = "B"
3. Send A to 50% of list, B to 50%
4. Compare open rates and click rates on respective detail pages
5. Send the winning version to the remaining list
