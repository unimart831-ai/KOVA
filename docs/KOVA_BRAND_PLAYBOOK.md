## 2. BRAND VOICE DEFINITION

### Voice Attributes

| Dimension | Kova's Position | Never |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| **Tone** | Confident & approachable | Arrogant, salesy, desperate |
| **Language** | Clear, conversational, occasionally bold | Jargon-heavy, corporate-speak, generic |
| **Humor** | Sharp, observational, relatable | Dadj jokes, forced memes, sarcasm that punches down |
| **Emotion** | Passionate about the mission, empathetic about pain points | Cold, robotic, preachy |
| **Complexity** | Simple enough for non-tech people, smart enough for developers | Dumbed-down or unnecessarily academic |
| **Cultural** | Proudly African, naturally multilingual (English/Swahili/Sheng) | Performatively woke, stereotypical, or culturally detached |
| **CTA style** | Invitational ("Try this"), challenger ("Are you still doing it the old way?") | Pushy ("Buy now!"), manipulative scarcity |

### Voice Examples — What Kova Sounds Like

**On product capability:**
> "Your social media manager charges $500/month, works 8 hours, takes weekends off, and still asks what to post. Kova's 6 AI agents work 24/7, learn your brand's DNA, and cost less than lunch in Nairobi."

**On a win:**
> "First Instagram reel published via Kova yesterday. Zero human intervention: Research Agent found the trending topic → Create Agent wrote the script → Adapt Agent picked 7:14 PM (peak engagement) → Published automatically. The loop works."

**On a pain point:**
> "You didn't start your business to spend 3 hours a day writing captions. You started it because you saw a problem worth solving. Let the AI handle the captions. You handle the vision."

**On competition:**
> "Hootsuite gives you a dashboard. Buffer gives you a timer. Jasper gives you a writer. Kova gives you all six — Research, Create, Predict, Schedule, Engage, Learn — in one autonomous loop. For $1/month."

**On Africa:**
> "When we say 'built for Africa,' we don't mean 'adapted for Africa.' We mean: built in Nairobi, tested with Kenyan businesses, priced in KES, paid via M-Pesa, optimized for mobile, and designed for the entrepreneur running 5 things at once."

**Casual / community moments:**
> "Monday morning. Your competitors are staring at a blank content calendar. Your Kova Daily Brief already has 5 posts ready to approve, 3 trending topics in your niche, and optimal publishing times calculated. Sip your chai, approve, and move on. 🍵"

### Language Rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Use numbers for impact | "6 agents, 9 platforms, $1/month" not "multiple agents, many platforms, affordable" |
| Lead with the insight, not the feature | "After 30 days, Kova knows your audience better than you do" not "We have a Content DNA feature" |
| Concrete > abstract | "Published 147 posts last week across 9 platforms" not "We help businesses scale their social presence" |
| Short sentences for punchy claims | "Not a scheduler. Not a dashboard. An AI team." |
| Active voice always | "Kova researches trends and creates content" not "Trends are researched and content is created" |
| Use Swahili/Sheng naturally, never forced | "Kazi mode 🔥" is good. "Karibu to our platform, habari!" is cringe |
| Hashtags: purposeful, not decorative | Max 3-5 per post. Always include #AfricaTech on relevant content |
| Emojis: strategic accent, not decoration | Use 1-3 max per post. 🔥 💡 🚀 ✅ are on-brand. 🥰 😍 💕 are not |

### Banned Words & Phrases

Never use these — they're generic, overused, or off-brand:

| Banned | Use Instead |
|--------|-------------|
| "Revolutionize" | "Replace" or "rebuild" |
| "Cutting-edge" | Be specific about what's new |
| "Leverage" | "Use" |
| "Synergy" | Just... no |
| "World-class" | Show it, don't claim it |
| "Game-changer" | Describe the actual change |
| "Empower" | "Give you" or "unlock" |
| "Seamless" | Describe the actual experience |
| "Unlock your potential" | "Stop wasting time on [specific thing]" |
| "We're excited to announce" | Just announce it. "New:" or "Shipped:" |
| "Stay tuned" | Give a specific date or action |

## 4. CONTENT STRATEGY & PILLARS

### The 5 Content Pillars

Every post Kova publishes should map to one of these pillars:

#### Pillar 1 — PRODUCT (30% of content)
**Goal**: Show what Kova does and drive signups

- Feature demos and walkthroughs
- New feature announcements ("Shipped:")
- Side-by-side comparisons (Kova vs manual, Kova vs competitors)
- Screen recordings of the AI agents working
- "How it works" explainers
- Pricing breakdowns and value propositions

**Key framing**: Always show the OUTCOME, not just the feature. Not "We added Content DNA" but "Kova now learns which of your posts get the most engagement and creates more like them."

#### Pillar 2 — BUILD IN PUBLIC (25% of content)
**Goal**: Build trust, authenticity, and founder community

- What we shipped this week
- Technical decisions and trade-offs
- Bug stories and lessons learned
- Metrics and milestones (users, posts published, agent accuracy)
- The real startup journey — wins AND struggles
- Stack and architecture insights

**Key framing**: Honest, specific, educational. Never brag without receipts. "We hit 1,000 posts published this week" with actual numbers.

#### Pillar 3 — SOCIAL MEDIA INTELLIGENCE (20% of content)
**Goal**: Position Kova as the authority on social media strategy

- Platform algorithm insights
- Content strategy tips (not generic — data-backed from Content DNA)
- "Most businesses do X wrong. Here's why."
- Trend analysis and predictions
- Format/timing/engagement best practices
- Myth-busting (e.g., "Posting every day doesn't help if your content sucks")

**Key framing**: Teach people things that make them go "I didn't know that." Become their go-to source for social media intelligence.

#### Pillar 4 — AFRICA TECH & MISSION (15% of content)
**Goal**: Rally the community, attract investors, inspire builders

- "Built for Africa" stories
- African entrepreneurship spotlights
- The tech-for-Africa opportunity and why it matters now
- M-Pesa and mobile-first innovation
- Hot takes on the African tech ecosystem
- "44 million MSMEs" narrative — humanize the number

**Key framing**: Proud but not preachy. Celebrating African innovation without being performative about it.

#### Pillar 5 — COMMUNITY & ENGAGEMENT (10% of content)
**Goal**: Build relationships, encourage conversation, create advocates

- Questions and polls
- User spotlights and testimonials (when available)
- Engagement bait that's actually valuable ("What's your biggest social media struggle?")
- Behind-the-scenes (office, team, daily routine)
- Celebrations (milestones, holidays, team wins)
- Reposts and comments on community content

**Key framing**: Make followers feel like insiders, not an audience. They're part of the Kova story.
