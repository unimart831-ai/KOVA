Understanding Your AI Agents

Kova Agent runs on a multi-agent architecture. Instead of one AI doing everything, you have a crew of specialized agents that collaborate — each an expert in their domain.

The Agent Crew

1. Research Agent (The Scout)

Scans trending topics, monitors your industry, and surfaces content opportunities. It feeds ideas to the Create Agent and keeps you ahead of trends.

  • What it does: Trend research, topic discovery, opportunity briefs
  • Available on: Starter plan and above

2. Create Agent (The Writer)

Your primary content creator. Takes seeds (ideas) and generates platform-native posts that match your brand voice. Learns from your approvals and rejections.

  • What it does: Content generation, platform adaptation, voice matching
  • Available on: Starter plan and above

3. Adapt Agent (The Optimizer)

Handles scheduling intelligence. Analyzes when your audience is most active and adjusts posting times to maximize engagement.

  • What it does: Smart scheduling, timing optimization, frequency management
  • Available on: Growth plan and above

4. Engage Agent (The Relationship Manager)

Monitors incoming comments and DMs across platforms. Drafts contextual replies that you can approve or let auto-send.

  • What it does: Comment monitoring, reply drafting, community management
  • Available on: Pro plan and above

5. Analyst Agent (The Strategist)

Analyzes your content performance and generates actionable insights — not just charts, but decisions. Identifies what's working, what's not, and why.

  • What it does: Performance analysis, content recommendations, insight generation
  • Available on: Growth plan and above

6. Chief Strategist (The Orchestrator)

Coordinates all agents and delivers your Daily Brief. It's the brain that ensures all agents work together toward your goals.

  • What it does: Agent orchestration, daily briefing, strategic planning
  • Available on: All plans

How Agents Work Together

Agents run autonomously on a schedule (via background tasks). A typical workflow:

  1. Research Agent finds a trending topic in your industry
  2. Chief Strategist decides it's relevant to your content pillars
  3. Create Agent generates posts about it for each platform
  4. Adapt Agent schedules them at optimal times
  5. Analyst Agent measures performance after publishing
  6. Engage Agent handles any comments that come in
