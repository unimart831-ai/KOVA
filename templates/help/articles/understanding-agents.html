Understanding Your AI Agents

Kova runs a multi-agent crew — specialized AI teammates that collaborate. All six agents are included on the Kova plan.

The agent crew

1. Research Agent (The Scout)

Scans trends, monitors your industry, and surfaces opportunities for your Daily Brief and campaign proposals.

2. Create Agent (The Writer)

Builds full marketing campaigns from your ideas — platform posts, carousels, reels, and commerce copy. Learns from your approvals and rejections.

3. Adapt Agent (The Optimizer)

Schedules posts when your audience is most active and adjusts timing based on performance.

4. Engage Agent (The Relationship Manager)

Monitors comments and WhatsApp conversations. Drafts replies for medium-confidence messages; you approve before send (or enable auto-reply in Settings).

5. Analyst Agent (The Strategist)

Turns performance data into decisions — what worked, what to do next, and revenue attribution.

6. Chief Strategist (The Orchestrator)

Coordinates the crew and delivers your Daily Brief each morning.

Typical workflow

  1. Research finds a trend in your niche
  2. Chief Strategist adds it to your brief as a campaign opportunity
  3. You pick an angle → Create builds the campaign package
  4. Adapt schedules approved posts
  5. Analyst measures results; Engage handles replies

Managing agents

Visit Agents to toggle agents, view activity, and add custom instructions. Use Emergency pause in Settings to halt all autonomous actions instantly.
