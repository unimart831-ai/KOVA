Understanding Your AI Agents

Kova runs specialized AI agents in the background. There is no separate agent control panel — you steer them from Settings and see outcomes on Home and in Studio.

What the agents do

  • Research — Surfaces trends and campaign ideas for your brief
  • Create — Builds campaign packages (posts, carousels, reels) in your brand voice
  • Adapt — Suggests timing and schedules approved posts
  • WhatsApp replies — Drafts or auto-sends inbox replies based on your WhatsApp reply style in Settings
  • Analyst — Turns performance into insights on Home and Results
  • Chief Strategist — Coordinates the crew and delivers your daily brief

Typical workflow

  1. Research or Strategist puts an opportunity on Home
  2. You open Studio and pick an angle — Create builds the package
  3. You approve (or auto-approve) → Adapt schedules → posts publish to WA / FB / IG
  4. Analyst measures results; WhatsApp AI helps with inbox replies when enabled

Where you control things

  • Settings — Brand voice, auto-approve, WhatsApp reply style, autopilot toggles
  • Home — Daily brief and next actions
  • Studio — Review, edit, approve, and publish
  • Emergency pause — In Settings, halt autonomous actions instantly
