Content Calendar & Scheduling

The Timeline (Content Calendar) gives you a visual overview of all your scheduled and published content across platforms and dates.

What You See

  • Scheduled posts — Posts approved and waiting to be published, shown at their scheduled time
  • Published posts — Posts that have already gone live, with platform and timestamp
  • Draft posts — Posts still in the creation pipeline

How Scheduling Works

You don't need to pick posting times manually. Here's the flow:

  1. You approve a post in the Queue
  2. The Adapt Agent analyzes your audience's engagement patterns and picks the optimal time
  3. The post appears on the Timeline at its scheduled slot
  4. At the scheduled time, Kova publishes the post via the platform's API

Smart Scheduling

The Adapt Agent doesn't just pick random times. It considers:

  • Historical engagement — When your audience is most active
  • Platform best practices — Optimal posting frequency per platform
  • Content type — Different content types perform better at different times
  • Spacing — Avoids posting too frequently or creating content gaps

Posting Frequency

You set your desired posting frequency (posts per week) during onboarding or in Settings. The Adapt Agent uses this as a target to distribute content evenly across the week.

What Happens When a Post Fails

If publishing fails (expired token, API error), the post is marked as Failed. You'll see it in the Queue with the option to retry. Check your Platforms page to ensure the connection is still active.
