Analytics & Performance Insights

The Insights page gives you AI-powered analytics — not just charts, but actionable recommendations about what to do next.

Key Metrics

  • Engagement Rate — Likes, comments, and shares relative to your reach
  • Reach — How many unique people saw your content
  • Best Performing Posts — Posts ranked by engagement, with insights on why they worked
  • Platform Breakdown — Performance comparison across your connected platforms

AI Insights

The Analyst Agent goes beyond numbers. It identifies patterns and generates insights like:

  • "Your question-based posts get 3x more comments than statement posts"
  • "Tuesday mornings consistently outperform other time slots"
  • "Posts about customer success stories have the highest share rate"

Acting on Insights

Each insight comes with a suggested action. You can:

  • Acknowledge — Mark the insight as reviewed
  • Apply — Let the AI adjust your strategy based on the insight
  • Dismiss — If the insight isn't relevant

Engagement Trends

The engagement trend chart shows your performance over time, helping you spot:

  • Growth or decline patterns
  • Impact of strategy changes
  • Seasonal or day-of-week patterns

Tips

  • Review insights weekly at minimum — they inform your content strategy
  • Look for patterns, not individual post performance — the AI helps surface these
  • Use insights to refine your brand voice and content pillars in Settings
