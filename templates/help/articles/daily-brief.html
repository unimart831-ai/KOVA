Your Daily Brief

The Daily Brief is your morning intelligence report — a summary of everything that happened with your social media, delivered by the Chief Strategist agent.

What's in the Brief

  • Performance Summary — Key metrics from the last 24 hours: engagement, reach, new followers
  • Top Performing Content — Which posts did best and why
  • Trending Topics — Relevant trends the Research Agent spotted in your industry
  • Content Seeds — AI-suggested post ideas based on trends and what's working
  • Agent Activity — What each agent did since your last brief
  • Recommendations — Strategic suggestions: what to post next, which platforms to focus on

When It's Delivered

Your Daily Brief is generated at your configured Daily Brief Time (set during onboarding or in Settings). The default is 8:00 AM in your timezone.

How to Use It

  1. Visit Daily Brief from the sidebar — it's the first item under Overview
  2. Read the performance summary to understand yesterday's results
  3. Review suggested content seeds — click on any to turn it into a post in Content Studio
  4. Check trending topics to decide if any are worth creating content about

Making the Most of Your Brief

  • Check it daily — consistency compounds. The AI gets smarter about what to highlight as it learns your preferences
  • Act on content seeds — they're generated based on what's working for you and what's trending in your industry
  • Use trends for timely content — the Research Agent surfaces trends early, giving you a first-mover advantage
