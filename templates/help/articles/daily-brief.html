Your Daily Brief

The Daily Brief is your morning intelligence report — performance, trends, and campaign opportunities, delivered by the Chief Strategist.

What's in the brief

  • Performance summary — Engagement, reach, and followers from the last 24 hours
  • Top content — What performed best and why
  • Trending topics — Relevant trends the Research Agent spotted
  • Campaign opportunities — Suggested angles you can turn into a full campaign in Studio
  • Agent activity — What each agent did since your last brief
  • Recommendations — What to focus on next

When it's delivered

Your brief is generated at your Daily Brief time (set during onboarding or in Settings). Default is 8:00 AM in your timezone. You can also receive a WhatsApp summary — reply IDEA 1 to start a campaign from a suggestion.

Owner WhatsApp commands

Reply to your morning brief on the owner number (or type HELP) for quick actions without opening the app:

  • APPROVE / APPROVE ALL — approve pending posts
  • LEADS / MONEY — hot leads and revenue snapshot
  • PRICE — list prices; PRICE shoes 2500 to update
  • STOCK — stock overview; STOCK shoes 10 or STOCK shoes OUT
  • SNAP — send a product photo to list and create content

How to use it

  1. Open Brief home from the sidebar
  2. Read yesterday's performance summary
  3. Tap a campaign opportunity → pick your angle on the proposals screen (uses 1 campaign)
  4. Check trends for timely content angles

Making the most of your brief

  • Check it daily — the AI learns what to highlight over time
  • Act on opportunities quickly — trends move fast
  • Use WhatsApp for on-the-go approvals and brief commands
