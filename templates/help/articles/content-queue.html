The Approval Queue

The Queue is your review station. Posts from approved campaigns land here before publishing (unless Auto-approve is on). You stay in control; Kova does the creative work.

How the queue works

  1. The Create Agent generates posts from your campaigns or Daily Brief suggestions
  2. Posts appear with status Pending review
  3. Read each post, check the platform, preview how it looks
  4. Approve to schedule, reject to discard, or edit before approving

Queue actions

  • Approve — Moves to Scheduled. The Adapt Agent picks the optimal posting time
  • Reject — Discards the post and helps the AI learn your preferences
  • Edit — Modify text, hashtags, or media before approving
  • Regenerate — Asks the Create Agent for a new version
  • Batch approve — Approve multiple posts from the same campaign at once

Post statuses

  • Pending — Waiting for your review
  • Approved / Scheduled — Waiting to publish at the scheduled time
  • Published — Live on the platform
  • Rejected — Discarded by you
  • Failed — Publishing failed (usually a token or API issue — reconnect from Platforms)

Tips

  • Review your queue daily — a 2–3 minute approve/reject routine is enough
  • Approve anything that's 80%+ good — consistency beats perfection
  • If you keep rejecting similar posts, update brand voice in Settings

Note: Publishing approved posts does not use extra campaigns — only starting a new campaign in Studio counts toward your monthly quota.
