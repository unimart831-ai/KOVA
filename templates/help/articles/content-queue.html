The Approval Queue

The Queue is your review station. All AI-generated posts land here before publishing (unless Auto-Approve is enabled). This keeps you in control while the AI does the creative heavy lifting.

How the Queue Works

  1. The Create Agent generates posts from your seeds or from Research Agent suggestions
  2. Posts appear in the Queue with status Pending Review
  3. You review each post — read the content, check the platform, preview how it looks
  4. Approve to schedule, reject to discard, or edit before approving

Queue Actions

  • Approve — The post moves to Scheduled. The Adapt Agent picks the optimal posting time
  • Reject — The post is discarded. Helps the AI learn your preferences
  • Edit — Opens the post editor. Modify text, hashtags, or media before approving
  • Regenerate — Asks the Create Agent to write a new version
  • Batch Approve — Approve multiple posts from the same seed at once

Post Statuses

  • Pending — Waiting for your review
  • Approved / Scheduled — Approved and waiting to be published at the optimal time
  • Published — Successfully posted to the platform
  • Rejected — Discarded by you
  • Failed — Publishing failed (usually a token or API issue)

Tips

  • Review your queue daily — a morning routine of approve/reject takes 2-3 minutes
  • Don't perfect every post — approve anything that's 80%+ good. Consistency beats perfection on social media
  • If you keep rejecting similar posts, update your brand voice in Settings to steer the AI
