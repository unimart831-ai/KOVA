Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Do I need to connect a platform before I can use Kova?

No. During onboarding Kova researches your market and drafts campaign content while you finish setup. Connect a platform when you're ready to publish — your drafts wait in Studio.

How long does it take for the AI to learn my brand voice?

The Create Agent matches your voice from day one using your brand profile. It improves as you approve, edit, and reject — each action is a signal.

Can I use Kova for multiple brands?

Multi-brand and team seats are available on the Agency plan. Contact sales for Wakala onboarding.

Campaigns & content

What counts as a campaign?

One marketing campaign = one activation from Studio, Snap, or a brief idea. It typically produces a reel, carousel, platform posts, and ties to your commerce page. Approving and publishing those posts does not consume extra campaigns.

How many campaigns can I run per month?

30 on the Kova plan. Trial accounts get 5. Add Boost, Scale, or Burst packs from Billing if you need more.

Can I edit AI-generated posts before publishing?

Yes. Every post can be edited before or after approval. Open Studio or the Queue to modify copy, hashtags, or media.

What happens if I reject a post?

Rejected posts are discarded. The AI learns from your patterns. If a style keeps missing, update brand voice in Settings.

Agents

Do agents run when I'm not using the app?

Yes. Research, Adapt, and Engage run on schedules. You approve what matters in Studio and your Daily Brief.

Can I turn off agents I don't want?

Yes. Visit Agents to pause any agent, or use Emergency pause in Settings.

Billing

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 7 days, 5 campaigns, full Kova features. No credit card required.

How does M-Pesa payment work?

Enter your phone on the Pricing page, confirm the STK push on your phone, and your subscription activates instantly.

What happens when my trial ends?

Subscribe to Kova (KSh 1,300/mo) to keep 30 campaigns/month. Without a subscription, agents pause and new campaigns cannot start — you can still log in and view your data.

Technical

Is my data secure?

Yes. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. All traffic is HTTPS. We never store social media passwords.

Which platforms are supported?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp Business inbox.

Can I use Kova on mobile?

Yes. Kova is a progressive web app — works in any browser. Reply to brief commands on WhatsApp from your phone.
