Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Do I need to connect a platform before I can use Kova?

You can explore the Content Studio and settings without connecting a platform, but to publish posts and receive engagement data you'll need at least one connected social account.

How long does it take for the AI to learn my brand voice?

The Create Agent starts matching your voice from day one using your brand voice description and examples. It gets better over time as you approve and reject posts — each action is a signal.

Can I use Kova for multiple brands?

Multi-brand management is available on the Agency plan. Each brand gets its own settings, agents, and content pipeline.

Content

How many posts can I create per day?

There's no hard limit on content creation. Your posting frequency setting determines how many posts get scheduled per week. You can create as many seeds as you want.

Can I edit AI-generated posts before publishing?

Yes. Every post can be edited before or after approval. Click Edit on any post in the Queue to modify the content, hashtags, or media.

What happens if I reject a post?

Rejected posts are discarded. The AI uses your rejection patterns to improve future content. If you consistently reject certain styles, update your brand voice description.

Can I upload my own images?

Yes. On the post editor, you can upload media (images or videos) to attach to any post.

Agents

Do agents run when I'm not using the app?

Yes. Agents run on scheduled intervals in the background. The Research Agent checks for trends, the Adapt Agent adjusts scheduling, and the Engage Agent monitors comments — all automatically.

Can I turn off agents I don't want?

Absolutely. Go to Agents and toggle off any agent you want to pause. This doesn't affect your plan — you can re-enable them anytime.

What are agent instructions?

Custom instructions you give to each agent to shape their behavior. For example, telling the Create Agent to "always include a call-to-action" or the Research Agent to "focus on fintech trends."

Billing

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade from the Billing page. Changes apply at the end of your current billing period.

Is there a free trial?

New accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card required.

How does M-Pesa payment work?

If you're in Kenya, you can pay via M-Pesa. Enter your phone number, confirm the amount on your phone, and the payment is processed instantly.

What happens when my trial ends?

You'll be prompted to choose a plan. If you don't subscribe, your account enters a limited state — you can still log in and view data, but agents stop running and no new posts are published.

Technical

Is my data secure?

Yes. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using Fernet encryption. All traffic is HTTPS. We never store your social media passwords.

Which social platforms are supported?

Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Pinterest. More platforms are coming soon.

Can I access Kova on mobile?

Kova is a progressive web app (PWA) — it works on any device with a browser. On mobile, you can add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.
