Engagement Inbox

The Engagement Inbox is your unified view of comments, DMs, and WhatsApp conversations — included on Kova.

How it works

  1. The Engage Agent monitors published posts and your WhatsApp inbox
  2. Incoming messages appear with sentiment (positive, neutral, negative)
  3. The agent drafts suggested replies
  4. You approve, edit, or write your own — or enable auto-reply for high-confidence messages

WhatsApp AI drafts

For customer WhatsApp messages, Kova drafts replies when confidence is medium. Approve from the Inbox or reply APPROVE REPLY on your owner brief number. Low-confidence messages always wait for you.

Sentiment analysis

Comments are tagged positive, neutral, or negative so you can prioritize complaints and questions.

Auto-engage

In Settings, enable Auto-engage to send replies to positive and neutral comments automatically. Negative comments always require your review.

Sending replies

  • Click a message to see full context and the AI draft
  • Edit if needed, then Send
  • Replies post directly via platform APIs

Use Emergency pause in Settings to halt all autonomous replies instantly.
