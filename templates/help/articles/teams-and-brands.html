Teams & Multi-Brand Management

Collaborate with team members and manage multiple brands from one account — available on the Agency plan. Contact sales for Wakala onboarding.

Creating a team

  1. Go to Teams
  2. Click Create team
  3. Name your team and add a description
  4. Invite members

Team roles

  • Owner — Full control including billing
  • Admin — Manage members, brands, and content (no billing)
  • Editor — Create, edit, and approve content
  • Viewer — Read-only access to content and analytics

Inviting members

Enter an email and role on your team page. Invitees get a join link. They need a Kova account — new users are prompted to sign up.

Multi-brand management

Each brand has its own voice, connected platforms, campaign pipeline, and analytics.

  • Brand voice and content pillars
  • Connected social accounts
  • Campaigns → posts → publishing
  • Analytics and engagement data

Switching brands

Use the brand selector in the sidebar. All content and analytics filter to the selected brand.
