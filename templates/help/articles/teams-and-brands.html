Teams & Multi-Brand Management

Kova's team features let you collaborate with team members and manage multiple brands from one account — available on the Biashara (Pro) plan.

Creating a Team

  1. Go to Teams from the sidebar
  2. Click Create Team
  3. Give your team a name and description
  4. Start inviting members

Team Roles

  • Owner — Full control. Can manage billing, members, brands, and all settings
  • Admin — Can manage members, brands, and content. Cannot access billing
  • Editor — Can create, edit, and approve content. Cannot manage members or settings
  • Viewer — Read-only access to content and analytics. Cannot create or modify anything

Inviting Members

On your team detail page, enter the email address and select a role. The invitee receives an email with a link to join. They'll need a Kova account — if they don't have one, they'll be prompted to sign up.

Multi-Brand Management

Each team can have multiple brands. A brand has its own:

  • Brand voice and content pillars
  • Connected social platforms
  • Content pipeline (seeds → posts → publishing)
  • Analytics and engagement data

Adding a Brand

  1. Go to your team detail page
  2. Under the Brands section, click Add Brand
  3. Configure the brand's voice, audience, and content pillars
  4. Connect the brand's social accounts

Switching Between Brands

If you manage multiple brands, use the brand selector in the sidebar to switch context. All content, analytics, and engagement data will filter to the selected brand.

Team Limits by Plan

  • Biashara (Pro) — Up to 5 team members, multiple brands
