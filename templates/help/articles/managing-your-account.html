Managing Your Account

The Settings page is your control center for profile, brand, and preference management.

Profile Settings

  • Full Name — Your display name across the app
  • Email — Your login email (also used for notifications)
  • Avatar — Upload a profile photo
  • Timezone — Used for scheduling, Daily Brief timing, and analytics
  • Daily Brief Time — When the Strategist delivers your morning brief

Brand Settings

  • Company Name — Your brand or business name
  • Website URL — Your primary website
  • Industry — Helps agents understand your context
  • Brand Voice — Detailed description of your communication style
  • Brand Voice Examples — Sample posts the AI learns from
  • Target Audience — Who you're creating content for
  • Content Pillars — Your 3-5 core content themes

Preferences

  • Posting Frequency — Target number of posts per week
  • Auto-Approve Posts — Skip the approval queue and let posts publish automatically
  • Auto-Engage — Let the Engage Agent reply to comments automatically (positive and neutral only)

Security

  • Change your password through the password reset flow
  • OAuth tokens for connected platforms are encrypted at rest
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS

Deleting Your Account

To delete your account, contact support@kovaagent.com. We'll remove all your data within 72 hours.
