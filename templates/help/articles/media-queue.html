Media Queue

The Media Queue is your visual content library — upload, organize, and reorder media files before attaching them to posts.

What Is the Media Queue?

Think of it as a staging area for your images and videos. Instead of uploading media one-by-one when creating posts, you can batch-upload everything into the queue and assign them later.

How to Use It

  1. Go to Media Queue from the sidebar under Content
  2. Upload media — Drag and drop files or click to browse. Supports images (JPG, PNG, WebP) and videos (MP4, MOV)
  3. Reorder — Drag items to rearrange the order. This determines which media gets used for the next post
  4. Assign to posts — When creating or editing a post, select media from your queue

Drag & Drop Reordering

The queue supports visual drag-and-drop reordering. Simply grab any media item and move it to your preferred position. The order is saved automatically.

Tips

  • Upload a week's worth of images at once — the Create Agent can pair them with generated posts
  • Use descriptive filenames — they help you identify media quickly in the queue
  • Keep the queue lean — remove media you've already used to stay organized
