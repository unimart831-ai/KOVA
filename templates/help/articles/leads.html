Lead Capture & Management

Kova's lead capture tools help you turn social media engagement into business opportunities. Collect leads from bio links, content CTAs, and engagement conversations — all tracked in one inbox.

How Leads Are Captured

  • Kova Links — Visitors who click through your bio link page can be tracked as leads
  • Content CTAs — Posts with call-to-action buttons can direct users to lead capture forms
  • Engagement conversations — The Engage Agent can flag high-intent comments (e.g., "how much?" or "do you deliver?") as potential leads
  • Kova Pixel — Website visitors who interact with your content after clicking a social post

Viewing Leads

  1. Go to Lead Inbox from the sidebar under Conversion
  2. See all captured leads with their source, platform, and status
  3. Click on a lead to view their full journey: which post they saw, what they clicked, and any engagement history

Lead Statuses

  • New — Just captured, needs review
  • Contacted — You've reached out
  • Qualified — Confirmed as a real opportunity
  • Converted — Became a customer
  • Lost — Not interested or unresponsive

Attribution

Kova tracks the full journey from social content to lead capture. You can see which platform, which post, and which CTA generated each lead — helping you understand what's actually driving business results.

Tips

  • Follow up quickly — leads from social media are time-sensitive
  • Check lead sources weekly — double down on platforms and content types that generate the most leads
  • Use the Engage Agent to auto-flag buying signals in comments
