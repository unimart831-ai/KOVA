Plans & Pricing

Kova Agent offers 4 plans to fit different needs — from solo creators to full-service agencies.

Plan Comparison

Starter — $39/month

  • Up to 3 connected platforms
  • Research Agent + Create Agent
  • Content Studio & Queue
  • Daily Brief
  • Basic analytics

Growth — $79/month

  • Up to 8 connected platforms
  • All 5 AI agents (Research, Create, Adapt, Engage, Analyst)
  • Smart scheduling (Adapt Agent)
  • Full analytics & insights
  • Content calendar

Pro — $149/month

  • Up to 20 connected platforms
  • Everything in Growth, plus:
  • Engagement Inbox with auto-reply
  • Competitor tracking (up to 5 competitors)
  • Team collaboration (coming soon)
  • Priority support

Agency — $299/month

  • Unlimited platforms
  • Everything in Pro, plus:
  • Multi-brand management
  • Up to 20 competitor tracks
  • API access (coming soon)
  • White-label options (coming soon)
  • Dedicated support

Payment Methods

  • Stripe — Credit/debit cards, processed securely via Stripe
  • M-Pesa — Available for Kenyan users. Pay via your phone number

Changing Plans

Go to Billing to view your current plan and upgrade/downgrade. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

Free Trial

New accounts start with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card required. After the trial, you choose a plan to continue.
