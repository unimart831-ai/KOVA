Plans & Pricing

Kova Agent offers 3 plans — from solopreneurs getting started to full omnichannel businesses. New accounts get a 7-day free trial with full Kazi (Growth) features.

Plan Comparison

Jipange — KSh 499/month

For solopreneurs getting started.

  • 2 social accounts (e.g. Instagram + Facebook)
  • 15 AI posts/month
  • 5 content seeds/month
  • 2 AI agents (Create + Analyst)
  • Daily brief
  • Upload your own images

Posts are text-first — no AI image generation on this plan. You can always upload your own images.

Kazi — KSh 999/month

For growing brands. Most Popular.

  • 4 social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn)
  • 60 AI posts/month
  • 30 content seeds/month
  • 4 AI agents (Create, Analyst, Research, Adapt)
  • AI image generation
  • M-Pesa commerce checkout & Shopify
  • Email brief reports, engagement inbox, competitor tracking

Biashara — KSh 1,999/month

For serious marketers and teams.

  • All 5 platforms including WhatsApp
  • 150 AI posts/month
  • 60 content seeds/month
  • All 6 AI agents
  • Auto-approve publishing
  • Up to 5 team members
  • Multi-touch attribution & Kova Pixel

Payment Methods

  • M-Pesa — Pay directly via your phone number. Instant processing
  • Stripe — Credit/debit cards, processed securely via Stripe

Changing Plans

Go to Billing to view your current plan and upgrade or downgrade. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

Free Trial

New accounts start with a 7-day Kazi trial — commerce, AI images, Adapt agent, and 4 platforms included. No credit card required. After the trial, choose Jipange (KSh 499), Kazi (KSh 999), or Biashara (KSh 1,999) to continue.
