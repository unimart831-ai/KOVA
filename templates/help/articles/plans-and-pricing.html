Plans & Pricing

Kova Agent offers 4 tiers — from solopreneurs getting started to agency teams. New accounts get a 7-day Starter trial (Starter plan limits, no payment required).

Plan Comparison

Jipange / Starter — KSh 499/month (~$4)

For solopreneurs getting started.

  • 2 social accounts
  • 18 AI posts/month · 8 content seeds/month
  • 8 studio polish credits/month
  • 2 AI agents (Create + Analyst)
  • Daily brief · upload your own images (no AI image generation)

Kazi / Growth — KSh 1,499/month (~$11)

For growing brands. Most Popular.

  • 4 social platforms (no WhatsApp Business)
  • 60 AI posts/month · 30 content seeds/month
  • 30 studio polish credits · 50 AI images/month
  • 4 AI agents · M-Pesa commerce & Shopify
  • 50 WhatsApp marketing conversations/month
  • Email briefs, engagement inbox, competitor tracking

Biashara / Pro — KSh 2,999/month (~$22)

For serious marketers and teams.

  • All 5 platforms including WhatsApp Business
  • 150 AI posts/month · 60 content seeds/month
  • 100 studio polish credits · 100 AI images/month
  • All 6 AI agents · 300 WhatsApp marketing convos/month
  • Auto-approve publishing · up to 5 team members

Wakala / Agency — KSh 7,999/month (~$59)

For agencies managing multiple clients. Contact sales — not available via self-serve checkout. Requires agency approval.

  • 25 social accounts · 300 posts/month · 120 seeds/month
  • 150 studio polish credits · 200 AI images/month
  • 1,000 WhatsApp marketing conversations/month · 25 team seats

Payment Methods

  • M-Pesa — Pay directly via your phone number. Instant processing
  • Stripe — Credit/debit cards, processed securely via Stripe

Changing Plans

Go to Billing to view your current plan and upgrade or downgrade. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

Free Trial

New accounts start with a 7-day Starter trial — Starter-tier limits for 7 days. No credit card required. After the trial, choose Starter (KSh 499), Growth (KSh 1,499), or Pro (KSh 2,999) to continue.
