Plans & Pricing

Kova Agent offers 4 plans to fit different needs — from solopreneurs getting started to full-service agencies. All plans come with a 14-day free trial.

Plan Comparison

Jipange — KSh 299/month

For solopreneurs getting started.

  • 1 social account
  • 15 AI posts/month
  • 5 content seeds/month
  • 2 AI agents (Create + Analyst)
  • Daily brief
  • Upload your own images

Posts are text-first — no AI image generation on this plan. You can always upload your own images.

Kazi — KSh 999/month

For growing brands. Most Popular.

  • 3 social accounts
  • 60 AI posts/month
  • 30 content seeds/month
  • 4 AI agents (Create, Analyst, Research, Adapt)
  • Daily brief
  • AI image generation (for Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest)
  • Email brief reports
  • Engagement inbox
  • Competitor tracking

AI images are generated for platforms that require them. All other platforms post as text-first. You can upload your own images for any platform.

Biashara — KSh 1,999/month

For serious marketers.

  • 10 social accounts
  • 150 AI posts/month
  • 60 content seeds/month
  • All 6 AI agents
  • Daily brief
  • AI image generation (for Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest)
  • Email brief reports
  • Engagement inbox
  • Competitor tracking
  • Auto-approve publishing
  • Up to 5 team members

Wakala — KSh 2,999/month

For agencies & teams.

  • 25 social accounts
  • Unlimited AI posts/month
  • Unlimited content seeds/month
  • All 6 AI agents
  • Daily brief
  • AI image generation (for Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest)
  • Email brief reports
  • Engagement inbox
  • Competitor tracking
  • Auto-approve publishing
  • Up to 25 team members

Payment Methods

  • M-Pesa — Pay directly via your phone number. Instant processing
  • Stripe — Credit/debit cards, processed securely via Stripe

Changing Plans

Go to Billing to view your current plan and upgrade or downgrade. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

Free Trial

New accounts start with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. After the trial, choose a plan to continue.
