Competitor Tracking

Monitor what competitors post, how often, and how their audience responds — included on the Kova plan.

Adding competitors

  1. Go to Competitors in the Intelligence section
  2. Click Add competitor
  3. Enter their brand name and social handles
  4. The Research Agent starts monitoring their activity

What you can track

  • Content frequency — Posting cadence by platform
  • Engagement rates — How their audience responds
  • Content themes — Topics they focus on
  • Posting patterns — Timing and performance

Competitive landscape

The Landscape view compares all tracked competitors side by side — relative performance and strategy differences at a glance.

How Kova uses this data

  • Research Agent — Finds content gaps your competitors cover that you don't
  • Analyst Agent — Benchmarks your performance
  • Chief Strategist — Includes competitor activity in your Daily Brief
