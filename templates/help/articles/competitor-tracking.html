Competitor Tracking

The Competitor Tracking feature lets you monitor what your competitors are doing on social media — available on the Kazi (Growth) plan and above.

Adding Competitors

  1. Go to Competitors from the Intelligence section in the sidebar
  2. Click Add Competitor
  3. Enter their brand name and social handles
  4. The Research Agent starts monitoring their activity

What You Can Track

  • Content frequency — How often they post and on which platforms
  • Engagement rates — How their audience responds
  • Content themes — What topics they focus on
  • Posting patterns — When they post and how it performs

Competitive Landscape

The Landscape view gives you a side-by-side comparison of all your tracked competitors, showing relative performance and strategy differences.

How Kova Uses This Data

Competitor intelligence feeds into your agent crew:

  • The Research Agent identifies content gaps — topics your competitors cover that you don't
  • The Analyst Agent benchmarks your performance against competitors
  • The Chief Strategist includes competitor activity in your Daily Brief

Limits

  • Kazi (Growth) — Track competitors on connected platforms
  • Biashara (Pro) — Full competitor landscape + strategist integration
