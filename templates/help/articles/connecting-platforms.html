Connecting Your Social Platforms

Kova Agent connects to your social media accounts via OAuth — the same secure method used by all major platforms. Kova never stores your social media passwords.

Supported Platforms

  • Facebook — Pages and profiles
  • Instagram — Business and Creator accounts (via Facebook)
  • Twitter / X — Personal and business accounts
  • LinkedIn — Personal profiles and company pages
  • TikTok — Creator and business accounts
  • Pinterest — Business accounts

How to Connect

  1. Go to Platforms from the sidebar
  2. Click the Connect button next to the platform you want
  3. You'll be redirected to the platform's login page
  4. Authorize Kova Agent to access your account
  5. You'll be redirected back to Kova — the platform will show as Connected

Platform Limits by Plan

  • Starter — Up to 3 connected platforms
  • Growth — Up to 8 connected platforms
  • Pro — Up to 20 connected platforms
  • Agency — Unlimited platforms

Disconnecting a Platform

Go to Platforms, find the connected account, and click Disconnect. This revokes Kova's access. Any scheduled posts for that platform will be paused.

Troubleshooting

  • Connection failed? — Make sure you're logged into the correct social account in your browser before connecting
  • Token expired? — Disconnect and reconnect the platform to refresh your access
  • Instagram not showing? — Instagram requires a connected Facebook Page. Connect Facebook first, then connect Instagram
