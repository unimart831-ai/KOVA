Connecting Your Social Platforms

Kova Agent connects to your social media accounts via OAuth — the same secure method used by all major platforms. Kova never stores your social media passwords.

Supported Platforms

  • WhatsApp — Business inbox to close leads and take payments
  • Facebook — Pages and profiles
  • Instagram — Business and Creator accounts (via Facebook)
  • TikTok — Creator and business accounts
  • LinkedIn — Personal profiles and company pages

How to Connect

Platform connection is optional during signup. Your AI agency still researches your industry and drafts posts without a connected account. Connect when you want to publish or schedule from Kova.

  1. Go to Platforms from the sidebar
  2. Click the Connect button next to the platform you want
  3. You'll be redirected to the platform's login page
  4. Authorize Kova Agent to access your account
  5. You'll be redirected back to Kova — the platform will show as Connected

Special Connection Flows

  • LinkedIn Company Pages — After authorizing, you'll see a list of company pages you manage. Select the one you want Kova to post to.
  • Instagram — Requires a connected Facebook Page. Connect Facebook first, then connect Instagram.
  • WhatsApp — Connect your WhatsApp Business account to reply to buyers and close sales from the inbox.

Platform Limits by Plan

  • Jipange (Starter) — 2 social accounts
  • Kazi (Growth) — 4 social platforms
  • Biashara (Pro) — All 5 platforms + WhatsApp
