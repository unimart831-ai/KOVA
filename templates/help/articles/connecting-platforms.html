Connecting Your Social Platforms

Connecting platforms lets Kova publish approved campaigns and pull engagement data. You can finish onboarding and draft campaigns before connecting — publish when you're ready.

Supported platforms

  • Facebook — Pages (not personal profiles)
  • Instagram — Business or Creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page
  • LinkedIn — Personal or company pages
  • TikTok — Business accounts
  • WhatsApp Business — Inbox for customer conversations (separate from Kova's owner brief number)

How to connect

  1. Go to Platforms
  2. Click Connect on the platform you want
  3. Authorize Kova in the OAuth window — we never see your password
  4. Repeat for each account you want to publish to

Account limits

The Kova plan includes up to 4 social accounts. Agency plans support more — contact sales.

Magic Fill (Instagram)

During onboarding you can connect Instagram to auto-fill brand voice, audience, and tone from your profile instead of typing everything manually.

Troubleshooting

  • Instagram won't connect — Ensure it's a Business/Creator account linked to a Facebook Page
  • Token expired — Reconnect from Platforms; Kova will prompt you if publishing fails
  • Wrong account — Disconnect and reconnect with the correct profile
