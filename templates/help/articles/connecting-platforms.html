Connecting Your Platforms

Connecting channels lets Kova publish approved posts and pull basic performance data. You can finish onboarding and draft in Studio before connecting — publish when you’re ready.

Supported in V1

  • WhatsApp Business — Inbox for customer conversations
  • Facebook — Pages (not personal profiles)
  • Instagram — Business or Creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page

How to connect

  1. Go to Channels
  2. Click Connect on WhatsApp, Facebook, or Instagram
  3. Authorize Kova in the OAuth / Meta window — we never see your password
  4. Repeat for each account you want to publish to

Account limits

Your plan includes a fixed number of social accounts (see Billing). Connect only what you need for WA / FB / IG.

Magic Fill (Instagram)

During onboarding you can connect Instagram to auto-fill brand voice, audience, and tone from your profile.

Troubleshooting

  • Instagram won’t connect — Ensure it’s a Business/Creator account linked to a Facebook Page
  • Token expired — Reconnect from Channels; Kova will prompt you if publishing fails
  • Wrong account — Disconnect and reconnect with the correct profile
