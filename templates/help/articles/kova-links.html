Kova Links — Bio Link Pages

Kova Links lets you create a customizable bio link page — one URL that houses all your important links. Perfect for Instagram bios, TikTok profiles, and anywhere you need a single link.

Creating Your Link Page

  1. Go to Kova Links from the sidebar under Conversion
  2. Click Create Link Page
  3. Add a title, description, and profile image
  4. Start adding links — each with a title, URL, and optional icon

Link Types

  • Standard links — Website, blog, shop, portfolio, etc.
  • Social profiles — Links to your social media accounts with platform icons
  • Featured links — Highlighted links that stand out (great for promotions)

Customization

Each link page can be customized with:

  • Profile photo and display name
  • Bio text
  • Theme colors
  • Link ordering (drag to reorder)

Analytics

Every Kova Link page tracks:

  • Page views — How many people visited your link page
  • Click-through rates — Which links get the most clicks
  • Referral sources — Where visitors come from

Sharing Your Link Page

Your link page URL is kovaagent.com/l/your-username. Add it to your Instagram bio, Twitter profile, TikTok bio, or anywhere you want to drive traffic.

Tips

  • Put your most important link at the top — most visitors click the first one
  • Update links regularly — promote current campaigns, new content, or limited offers
  • Use the analytics to see what your audience cares about most
