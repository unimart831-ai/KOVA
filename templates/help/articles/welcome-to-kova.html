Welcome to Kova Agent

Kova Agent is an autonomous social intelligence platform — not just another scheduler. It uses a crew of 6 AI agents that work together to research trends, create content, optimize timing, engage your audience, analyze performance, and orchestrate your entire social media strategy.

How Kova is Different

Traditional tools make you do the work and add AI as a feature. Kova flips that — AI runs your social media while you stay in control. Your agents work 24/7 in the background, and you approve what matters.

Your AI Agent Crew

  • Research Agent (The Scout) — Monitors trends, tracks competitors, and surfaces content opportunities
  • Create Agent (The Writer) — Turns your ideas into platform-native posts that match your brand voice
  • Adapt Agent (The Optimizer) — Picks the best posting times and adjusts scheduling in real-time
  • Engage Agent (The Relationship Manager) — Monitors comments and crafts contextual replies
  • Analyst Agent (The Strategist) — Analyzes performance and delivers actionable insights
  • Chief Strategist — Orchestrates all agents and delivers your Daily Brief

Quick Start Checklist

  1. Connect at least one social platform — Go to Platforms and connect your accounts
  2. Set up your brand voice — Visit Settings to describe your tone, audience, and content pillars
  3. Create your first post — Head to Content Studio, type an idea, and let the AI generate multi-platform content
  4. Review your Daily Brief — Check Daily Brief each morning for performance updates and recommendations
  5. Enable your agents — Visit Agents to toggle on the agents you want working for you
