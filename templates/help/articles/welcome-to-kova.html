Welcome to Kova

Kova helps you create and publish social content for WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram — then shows what worked on Home and Results.

Your path in V1

  1. Sign up — Create your account and finish the short brand setup.
  2. Confirm your brand — Voice, audience, and business details in Settings (or during onboarding).
  3. Connect channels — Link WhatsApp, Facebook, and/or Instagram under Channels.
  4. Create in Studio — Open Studio, review draft campaigns, and edit what you need.
  5. Approve — Approve posts you’re happy with (or turn on auto-approve in Settings).
  6. Publish — Scheduled posts go live to your connected accounts.
  7. Check Home & Results — Home for today’s plan; Results for attributed revenue.

What you won’t see in V1

Teams, Engage inbox, LinkedIn, TikTok, and competitor tracking are out of scope. Focus on WA / FB / IG, Studio, and Results.
