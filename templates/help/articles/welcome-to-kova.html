Welcome to Kova Agent

Kova Agent is an autonomous social intelligence platform — not just another scheduler. Six AI agents work together to research trends, create content, optimize timing, engage your audience, analyze performance, and deliver your Daily Brief. You stay in control; the agents do the heavy lifting.

How Kova is Different

Traditional tools make you do the work and add AI as a feature. Kova flips that — AI runs your social media while you approve what matters. Your agents work in the background 24/7, and you see value within minutes of finishing setup.

Express Setup (about 2 minutes)

New accounts go through a short Kova Express flow — no long forms before you see results.

  1. What brings you here — Choose whether you want to sell products, grow on social, or both. Optionally paste a website or social link.
  2. About your business — Name, business name, industry, and optional phone or website. If you pasted a link, Kova reads it and pre-fills what it can.
  3. Confirm your brand — Review a preview card (voice, audience, tone, pillars). Tap Looks good — start my agency, or edit details later in Settings.
  4. Your agency starts — Research, content ideas, draft posts, and your welcome brief run automatically. When ready, head to Content Studio.

Three Ways In

  • Sell products — Best for shops and retailers. After setup, add your first product with Snap to Sell and share your shop link.
  • Grow on social — Connect Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn to auto-fill your brand from your profile (Magic Fill). Platform connect is optional until you want to publish.
  • Paste a link — Website or social URL on the welcome screen; Kova infers industry, voice, and audience from the page.

Note: Connecting a social account is not required to finish setup. Your AI agency still drafts content. Connect anytime from Platforms when you're ready to publish.

Your AI Agent Crew

  • Research Agent (The Scout) — Monitors trends, tracks competitors, and surfaces content opportunities
  • Create Agent (The Writer) — Turns ideas into platform-native posts that match your brand voice
  • Adapt Agent (The Optimizer) — Picks posting times and adjusts scheduling in real time
  • Engage Agent (The Relationship Manager) — Monitors comments and crafts contextual replies
  • Analyst Agent (The Strategist) — Analyzes performance and delivers actionable insights
  • Chief Strategist — Orchestrates all agents and delivers your Daily Brief

Setup Mission (first 14 days)

After onboarding, your Brief home shows a Setup mission progress bar until core milestones are done — brand ready, first posts generated, platform connected, first publish, and (for commerce industries) first product and shop link shared.

Quick Start Checklist

  1. Finish express setup — Complete the welcome flow and wait for your agency to finish (usually 1–3 minutes)
  2. Review draft posts — Open Content Studio and approve or edit AI-generated content
  3. Connect a platform — When ready to publish, go to Platforms and connect your accounts
  4. Refine brand voice — Optional tweaks in Settings (see Setting Up Your Brand Voice)
  5. Selling online? — Use Snap to Sell, then share your shop from Products
  6. Read your Daily Brief — Check Brief home each morning for trends and recommendations
