Using the Content Studio

The Content Studio is where ideas become posts. Type a content idea (called a "seed"), and Kova's Create Agent generates platform-native content for all your connected platforms.

How It Works

  1. Submit a seed idea — Type what you want to post about. This can be a topic, headline, rough draft, or even a URL
  2. AI generates posts — The Create Agent writes a unique version for each connected platform, matching your brand voice and each platform's best practices
  3. Review the results — Each generated post appears as a card you can preview, edit, or regenerate
  4. Approve or reject — Approved posts move to the scheduling queue. Rejected posts are discarded

Writing Good Seeds

The quality of your seed directly affects the output. Here are tips:

  • Be specific: "5 tips for remote team productivity" beats "something about remote work"
  • Include a hook: "The counterintuitive reason top CEOs wake up late" gives the AI a strong starting angle
  • Mention format: "Carousel post about our Q3 results" or "Thread about AI trends" helps the AI structure content
  • Add context: "We just launched X feature — announce it with excitement" gives purpose

Platform-Native Content

Each platform gets a tailored version:

  • Twitter/X — Concise, hook-driven, hashtag-optimized (280 chars)
  • LinkedIn — Professional tone, longer-form, thought leadership style
  • Instagram — Visual-first caption, strategic hashtags, engagement CTAs
  • Facebook — Conversational, community-focused, shareable
  • TikTok — Trending style, casual voice, relevant hashtags

Post Actions

  • Approve — Moves the post to the scheduling queue
  • Reject — Discards the post (the AI learns from rejections)
  • Regenerate — Creates a new version of the same seed
  • Edit — Manually modify the generated content before approving

Auto-Approve Mode

Tired of approving every post? Enable Auto-Approve in Settings. The AI will schedule posts automatically. Recommended once you're confident in your brand voice setup.
