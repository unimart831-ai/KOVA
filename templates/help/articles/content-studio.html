Using the Content Studio

Studio is where marketing campaigns start. Describe what you want to promote, pick your platforms, and Kova builds a full package — platform-native posts, carousel, reel, and commerce links — for your approval.

How it works

  1. Start a campaign — Type your idea, angle, or offer. Or use voice memo to dictate a brief.
  2. Kova builds the package — The Create Agent writes unique copy per platform, matching your brand voice.
  3. Review in Studio — Each output appears as a card you can preview, edit, or regenerate.
  4. Approve — Approved posts move to the queue for scheduling. Nothing publishes until you approve.

From Snap or the Daily Brief

You can also start campaigns from:

  • Snap to Sell — Photo → marketing proposals → pick an angle (uses 1 campaign)
  • Daily Brief — One-click on a suggestion → proposals screen → pick your angle
  • WhatsApp — Send a product photo or reply IDEA 1 to your morning brief

Writing strong campaign ideas

  • Be specific: "Weekend 20% off braids — book by Friday" beats "salon promo"
  • Include a hook: Give Kova a headline or angle to riff on
  • Mention format: "Carousel for new collection" or "Reel showing before/after"
  • Add context in notes: Audience, CTA, or things to avoid

Platform-native content

Each connected platform gets tailored copy — not copy-paste. Instagram gets visual captions; LinkedIn gets thought-leadership tone; TikTok gets casual hooks.

Campaign quota

The strip at the top of Studio shows campaigns left this month. Each activation uses one campaign from your quota (30 on Kova, 5 on trial). Publishing approved posts is unlimited.

Auto-approve

Once you trust your voice, enable Auto-approve in Settings so approved-quality posts schedule automatically.
