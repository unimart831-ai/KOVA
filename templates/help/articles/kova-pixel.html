Kova Pixel — Website Tracking

The Kova Pixel is a lightweight JavaScript snippet for your website. It tracks visitors and conversions and connects them back to your social campaigns.

What it does

  • Page views — Which pages your social audience visits
  • Conversions — Purchases, sign-ups, or custom events
  • Attribution — Links website activity to the post or platform that drove the visit
  • Revenue tracking — Social ROI when you fire conversion events with values

Setting up the pixel

  1. Go to Kova Pixel under Conversion
  2. Copy the snippet
  3. Paste into the <head> of your site (or Google Tag Manager)
  4. Page views start tracking immediately

Tracking custom events

// Track a purchase
KovaPixel.track('purchase', { value: 2500, currency: 'KES' });

// Track a sign-up
KovaPixel.track('signup');

Viewing results

On the Pixel page: total events, unique visitors, revenue attributed to social, and top pages.

How attribution works

When someone clicks from your social post, UTM parameters link their session to that campaign. You see which content drives real business results — included on Kova.
