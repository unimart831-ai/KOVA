Setting Up Your Brand Voice

Your brand voice is one of the most important settings in Kova. It tells the Create Agent how to write your content — the tone, style, and personality that makes your brand recognizable.

Where to Configure

During express onboarding, Kova pre-fills voice, audience, tone, and content pillars from your industry pack (and from your website or social profile if you connected one). You confirm on a preview card — no long form required.

After setup, go to Settings anytime to refine your brand. Most users tweak voice and pillars after reviewing their first week of AI-generated posts.

Brand Voice

Describe how your brand communicates. Be specific — the more detail you provide, the better the AI matches your style.

Good examples:

  • "Professional but approachable. We use simple language, avoid jargon, and occasionally add light humor. Think friendly expert, not corporate."
  • "Bold, confident, and direct. Short sentences. Strong opinions. We challenge conventional thinking in our industry."
  • "Warm and empathetic. We speak like a trusted friend who happens to be an expert. We use 'you' and 'we' a lot."

Target Audience

Describe who you're talking to. Include demographics, pain points, and what they care about.

Example: "Small business owners aged 28-45 who are active on social media but don't have time to create content consistently. They value authenticity over polish."

Content Pillars

Content pillars are the 3-5 core themes your content revolves around. They keep your content focused and on-brand.

Example for a fitness brand:

  • Workout tips & routines
  • Nutrition & meal prep
  • Mindset & motivation
  • Client transformations

Industry

Select your industry during onboarding or in Settings. This helps the Research Agent find relevant trends and the Create Agent use appropriate terminology.

Tips for Better Results

  • Review and refine your brand voice after your first week — see what the AI produces and adjust
  • Add brand voice examples in Settings — paste 2-3 of your best posts so the AI can learn from them
  • Keep content pillars to 3-5 topics — too many dilutes focus
  • Update your audience description as your business grows
