How Your Commission Rate Works

Your commission rate is determined by how many active clients you have at any given time. An "active client" is one who has paid for at least 2 consecutive months.

The Four Tiers

15%

1–25 active clients

Starting tier. Begin here and grow.

20%

26–75 active clients

Proven partner. Growing network.

25%

76–150 active clients

Catalyst. Driving serious growth.

30%

150+ active clients

Top tier. Maximum commission.

Tier Upgrades Apply to All Clients

When your tier increases, the new rate applies to all your active clients — not just new ones. If you jump from 15% to 20%, every client's commission is recalculated at 20%.

Real Earnings Examples

Side Hustle (15 clients, 6 months in)

  • 8 Starter (KES 99) + 5 Growth (KES 500) + 2 Pro (KES 1,500)
  • Monthly: (8 × 99 × 15%) + (5 × 500 × 15%) + (2 × 1,500 × 15%) = KES 943.80/month

Dedicated Partner (60 clients, 12 months in)

  • 20 Starter + 30 Growth + 10 Pro → 20% tier
  • Monthly: (20 × 99 × 20%) + (30 × 500 × 20%) + (10 × 1,500 × 20%) = KES 6,396/month

Growth Machine (200 clients, 18 months in)

  • 50 Starter + 100 Growth + 40 Pro + 10 Agency → 30% tier
  • Monthly: (50 × 99 × 30%) + (100 × 500 × 30%) + (40 × 1,500 × 30%) + (10 × 3,500 × 30%) = KES 40,485/month

The 24-Month Rule

Commission on each client runs for 24 months from their first payment. After that, commission for that specific client stops — but the client still counts toward your active total for tier purposes as long as they remain subscribed.

This means a partner with long-running clients maintains their tier even as individual commission windows close.

Plan Upgrades and Downgrades

If a referred client upgrades from Starter (KES 99) to Growth (KES 500), your commission automatically adjusts. Your next month's commission is based on KES 500 instead of KES 99. The reverse applies on downgrades.
