The Three Layers

The Kova Growth Partners Program isn't a simple referral programme. It's a three-layer earnings system designed to reward partners who build genuine, lasting relationships with their referrals.

Layer 1: Recurring Commission

Every month a referred client pays their Kova subscription, you earn a percentage of that payment. This isn't a one-time finder's fee — it's recurring income for up to 24 months per client.

Your commission rate starts at 15% and increases to 30% as you bring more active clients. The rate applies to all your clients, not just new ones.

  • 1–25 active clients: 15% commission
  • 26–75 active clients: 20% commission
  • 76–150 active clients: 25% commission
  • 150+ active clients: 30% commission

Layer 2: Milestone Bonuses

At key client milestones, you earn one-time cash bonuses and permanent benefits. These are cumulative — your progress never resets even if a client churns temporarily.

  • 10 clients: KES 2,500 + Ambassador badge
  • 25 clients: KES 10,000 + commission upgrade to 20%
  • 50 clients: KES 50,000 + featured on Kova website
  • 100 clients: KES 200,000 + commission upgrade to 25% + profit share unlocked
  • 250 clients: KES 500,000 + commission upgrade to 30% + higher profit share

Layer 3: Profit Share

Elite partners with 100+ active clients (and strong retention) earn a share of Kova's quarterly net profits — on top of commission and bonuses. This is the "equity without equity" path.

  • 100+ clients (6-month avg retention): 1% of quarterly net profit
  • 200+ clients: 2% of quarterly net profit
  • 500+ clients: 3% of quarterly net profit + Advisory Board seat

How Referral Tracking Works

  1. You share your unique referral link (e.g., kovaagent.com/?ref=KOVA-JAMES-A3X7)
  2. When someone clicks the link and signs up, they're permanently tagged as your referral
  3. After 2 consecutive paid months, the client becomes "active" and commission begins
  4. Commission is calculated monthly on the client's actual subscription payment
  5. You get paid on the 15th of every month via M-Pesa or bank transfer

What Makes This Different

Most referral programmes offer a flat one-time payment. Kova's programme is designed for people who want to build real income streams:

  • Recurring, not one-time: You earn every month, not just at signup
  • Rate increases with scale: The more clients, the higher your rate on all clients
  • Three earning layers: Commission + bonuses + profit share
  • Transparent tracking: Real-time dashboard shows everything
  • No cap: There's no limit on how many clients you can refer or how much you can earn
