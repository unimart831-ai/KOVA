1

Apply

Head to the application page and fill out the form. We ask for:

  • Your name and email
  • Your company or brand (optional)
  • A description of your audience and how you plan to share Kova
You don't need to be an existing Kova customer. Anyone with a relevant audience can apply.
2

Get Approved

We review every application within 48 hours. We're looking for partners who:

  • Have a genuine audience (social media, email list, community, clients)
  • Understand what Kova does and who it's for
  • Plan to recommend Kova authentically, not through spam

Once approved, you'll receive an email with your unique referral code and a link to your Partner Dashboard.

3

Set Up Your Dashboard

Your Partner Dashboard is where you track everything:

Referral code & shareable link
Pending & active clients
Earnings & payout history
Milestone progress
4

Share Your Link

Use your personalized referral link wherever you talk about social media management:

  • Social media: Post about your experience, share tips, mention Kova
  • Blog/YouTube: Write reviews, tutorials, comparisons
  • Client conversations: Recommend to prospects who need automation
  • Email: Include in newsletters or direct outreach
  • Communities: Share in relevant groups (where allowed)

What Works Best

Our most successful partners share from genuine experience. Instead of "Sign up for this tool", they say:

"Here's how I automated my social media and got better results"

"My clients used to spend 2 hours daily on social media. Now it's 5 minutes."

"I tested 5 social media AI tools. Here's why I recommend Kova."

5

Track Your Earnings

Here's the timeline from referral to payout:

  1. Day 1: Someone signs up using your link → appears as "Pending" in your dashboard
  2. Month 1: They make their first payment → still pending
  3. Month 2: Second consecutive payment → "Active", commission begins
  4. Month 3, 15th: Your first commission payout for this client arrives
  5. Every month after: Commission continues automatically for 24 months
6

Grow and Unlock More

As your active client count grows:

  • 10 clients: First milestone bonus (KES 2,500)
  • 26 clients: Commission rate jumps to 20%
  • 100 clients: Profit share unlocked
  • 250+ clients: Maximum rates, maximum bonuses, Advisory Board eligibility

Tips for Success

Use Kova yourself

The best recommendations come from real experience.

Target the right audience

Business owners, freelancers, and marketers who struggle with consistent social media.

Be patient

Commission starts after the 2-month activation. Focus on quality referrals.

Create content

Reviews, tutorials, and comparison posts drive long-term referrals.
