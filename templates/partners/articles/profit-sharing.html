The Elite Tier

Profit sharing is the third and most lucrative layer of the Growth Partners Program. It's reserved for partners who demonstrate they can drive sustained, high-quality growth — not just signups, but clients who stay and thrive.

Qualification Requirements

To unlock profit sharing, you need:

  1. 100+ active referred clients (paid for 2+ consecutive months)
  2. Average 6-month retention across your referred clients
  3. A signed annual partnership agreement

What Is "Net Profit"?

Net profit = total revenue minus all operating costs (hosting, AI APIs, payment processing fees, salaries, marketing, etc.) and taxes. This is the real bottom line, not just revenue.

How It's Paid

  • Profit share is calculated and paid quarterly
  • Payouts happen within 30 days of the quarter ending
  • You receive a breakdown showing the revenue, costs, net profit, and your share

Safeguards

  • Pool cap: Maximum 10 partners can have profit share at any time. Total pool is capped at 15% of net profit.
  • Retention requirement: Your referred clients must have a 6-month average retention. This prevents mass-signup gaming.
  • Quarterly review: If your active count drops below the threshold for 2 consecutive quarters, profit share pauses until you're back above.
  • Annual agreement: Requires a signed partnership contract reviewed annually.

Stacking All Three Layers

At the profit share level, you're earning from all three layers simultaneously:

  • Layer 1: 25–30% monthly commission on every client payment
  • Layer 2: Milestone bonuses already received (KES 762,500 total if at 250+)
  • Layer 3: 1–3% of quarterly net profits

This is the "equity without equity" model — real profit participation without the complexity of actual equity ownership.
