What Are Milestone Bonuses?

Milestone bonuses are one-time cash payouts awarded when you reach specific active client counts. They're designed to reward sustained effort and commitment to growing the Kova community.

Key Rules

  • Cumulative progress: Your progress toward milestones never resets. If a client churns and your count dips from 28 to 24, you don't lose the 25-client milestone.
  • Payment timeline: Bonuses are paid within 14 days of achieving the milestone.
  • Rate overrides: If a milestone gives you a higher commission rate than your current tier, the milestone rate takes effect permanently.

Stacking with Commission

Milestone bonuses are in addition to your recurring monthly commission. When you hit the 50-client milestone, you receive KES 50,000 cash AND continue earning your monthly commission at 20% or higher.

Total Bonus Potential

If you hit all five milestones, you earn a total of KES 762,500 in one-time bonuses — on top of your monthly commission and potential profit share.
