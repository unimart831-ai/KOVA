1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) supplements our Terms & Conditions and outlines what constitutes prohibited use of the Kova Agent platform. All users, team members, and API consumers must comply with this policy.

2. Prohibited Content

You may not use Kova to create, distribute, or promote content that:

  • Is illegal, fraudulent, or deceptive in any jurisdiction.
  • Promotes hate, discrimination, or violence against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other characteristic.
  • Contains or distributes child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Infringes intellectual property rights (copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets).
  • Constitutes harassment, bullying, doxxing, or threats.
  • Contains malware, viruses, or other harmful code.
  • Promotes self-harm, suicide, or dangerous activities.
  • Is sexually explicit or pornographic.
  • Impersonates real individuals, brands, or organizations without authorization.
  • Spreads deliberate misinformation or disinformation.

3. Prohibited Activities

You may not use the Service to:

  • Spam or bulk messaging — sending unsolicited bulk messages, comments, or DMs on connected platforms.
  • Fake engagement — artificially inflating likes, followers, comments, or other metrics.
  • Platform manipulation — coordinated inauthentic behavior, astroturfing, or manipulating social platform algorithms.
  • Scraping or data harvesting — using the Service or its APIs to scrape data from social platforms beyond what is necessary for normal Service operation.
  • Circumventing limits — creating multiple accounts, using proxies, or other methods to bypass plan limits or rate limits.
  • Reverse engineering — decompiling, disassembling, or reverse-engineering any part of the Service.
  • Unauthorized access — accessing other users’ accounts, data, or connected social accounts without permission.
  • Competitive extraction — using the Service primarily to build a competing product.

4. AI-Specific Guidelines

When using Kova’s AI features:

  • Do not attempt to manipulate AI agents into producing prohibited content through prompt injection or jailbreaking.
  • Do not present AI-generated content as factual reporting or journalism without human verification.
  • You are responsible for reviewing and approving all AI-generated content before publication.
  • Do not use AI-generated content to deceive audiences about its origin where disclosure is required by law or platform policies.

5. Platform Compliance

You must comply with the terms of service and community guidelines of every social media platform you connect to Kova. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Community Standards
  • X (Twitter) Rules
  • LinkedIn Professional Community Policies
  • TikTok Community Guidelines
  • YouTube Community Guidelines
  • Pinterest Community Guidelines

Violations of connected platform policies that result from your use of Kova are your responsibility.

6. Enforcement

Violations may result in:

  • Warning — first notice for minor infractions.
  • Feature restriction — temporary suspension of specific features (e.g., auto-publish, engagement).
  • Account suspension — temporary or permanent suspension with or without notice for serious violations.
  • Legal action — referral to law enforcement for illegal activities.

We reserve the right to remove content and restrict accounts at our discretion to protect the platform and its users.

7. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or activity that violates this policy, please report it to abuse@kovaagent.com.
