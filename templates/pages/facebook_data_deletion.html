Overview

Kova Agent (“Kova”) connects to Facebook and Instagram through Meta’s APIs when you authorize our app. If you remove Kova from your Facebook settings or request deletion of data we received from Meta, we disconnect your linked accounts and delete the associated tokens and profile metadata stored in Kova.

This page satisfies Meta’s requirement for a Data Deletion Instructions URL. Our automated callback is registered at {{ site_url }}/platforms/facebook/data-deletion/.

Option 1 — Remove the app in Facebook

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and Websites → Active.
  3. Select Kova Agent (or the name of our Meta app) → Remove.
  4. Confirm removal. Meta notifies Kova automatically; we clear your Facebook/Instagram connection data and OAuth tokens within minutes.

After removal, Meta may show you a confirmation link on Kova with a status code — that page confirms we processed the request.

Option 2 — Disconnect inside Kova

  1. Sign in at {{ site_url }}/.
  2. Open Platforms ({{ site_url }}/platforms/).
  3. For each connected Facebook or Instagram account, choose Disconnect.

Disconnecting clears stored access tokens immediately. Historical posts and analytics in Kova may be retained while your subscription is active so you can reference past performance; published content on Facebook/Instagram remains on those networks.

Option 3 — Delete your full Kova account

To delete your entire Kova account and all personal data we hold (not only Meta-sourced data), email {{ support_email }} from the address on your account. We complete deletion within 72 hours as described in our Privacy Policy.

For privacy-specific requests (access, portability, erasure under GDPR), contact {{ privacy_email }}.

What we delete

  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens for Facebook and linked Instagram Business accounts
  • Facebook user ID, Page tokens, and Graph profile metadata stored in Kova
  • Facebook Login association (django-allauth link), if present
  • Active connection flags — accounts are deactivated so Kova cannot access Meta on your behalf

What we may retain

  • Billing and tax records as required by law
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics that cannot identify you
  • Content you published to social networks (it remains on those platforms under their policies)
  • Server security logs for up to 90 days

Questions

Email {{ support_email }} with your Kova account email and, if available, your Facebook confirmation code from Meta.
