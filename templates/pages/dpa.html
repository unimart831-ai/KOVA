1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the Terms & Conditions between Kova Technologies (“Processor”) and you (“Controller”) and applies where Kova processes personal data on your behalf through the Service.

This DPA is designed to help you meet compliance obligations under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable data protection laws.

2. Definitions

  • Personal Data — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that Kova processes on your behalf.
  • Processing — any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
  • Sub-processor — a third-party service provider that processes Personal Data on Kova’s behalf.

3. Scope of Processing

Kova processes the following categories of personal data on your behalf:

Data Category Data Subjects Purpose
Social media profile data Your followers, commenters Engagement monitoring, interaction tracking
Comments and messages Social media users Sentiment analysis, reply generation
Lead form submissions Your leads and prospects Lead capture, email marketing
Email addresses Your subscribers and clients Email marketing on your behalf

4. Obligations of the Processor

Kova shall:

  • Process Personal Data only on your documented instructions and for the purposes described in this DPA.
  • Ensure that personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures.
  • Notify you without undue delay upon becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.
  • Assist you in responding to data subject rights requests.
  • Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of the Service, unless retention is required by law.

5. Sub-processors

Kova uses the following categories of sub-processors:

Category Sub-processor Location
Cloud Infrastructure Railway (hosting), Cloudflare (R2 storage, CDN) United States
AI/LLM Processing OpenRouter (LLM API gateway) United States
Payment Processing Stripe, Safaricom (M-Pesa) United States / Kenya
Email Delivery Resend United States

We will notify you of any new sub-processors at least 14 days before they begin processing. You may object in writing within that period.

6. International Transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside Kenya or the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms approved by the relevant data protection authority.

7. Security Measures

Kova implements the following measures:

  • Encryption of OAuth tokens and sensitive fields at rest.
  • TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • CSRF protection and secure session management.
  • Access controls with role-based permissions for team features.
  • Regular security updates and dependency monitoring.
  • Automated backups of application databases.

8. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a Personal Data breach, Kova will:

  • Notify you without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware.
  • Provide details of the nature, scope, and consequences of the breach.
  • Describe the measures taken or proposed to address the breach.
  • Cooperate with your compliance and notification obligations.

9. Contact

For DPA-related inquiries: privacy@kovaagent.com
