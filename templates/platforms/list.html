{# ── Instagram Creator Tip ── #}

Instagram Personal Account?

Instagram's API requires a Business or Creator account. You can convert yours for free in 30 seconds:

  1. Open Instagram → Profile → three-line menu (≡) → Settings → Account type and tools
  2. Tap "Switch to professional account"
  3. Choose Creator (recommended) or Business
  4. Done! Now connect Instagram above
{# ── Facebook Personal Note ── #}

Note on Facebook: Facebook only allows posting to Pages via their API — personal profile posting was disabled by Meta in 2018. This applies to all third-party tools, not just Kova.

Multi-page businesses: If you manage multiple Facebook Pages, Kova publishes to the active page (shown with a blue check above). To switch the active page, disconnect and reconnect your Facebook account.
