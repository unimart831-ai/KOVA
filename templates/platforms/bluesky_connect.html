Bluesky uses App Passwords instead of OAuth. Create one in your Bluesky settings, then enter it below.

How to create an App Password:

  1. Go to bsky.app/settings/app-passwords
  2. Click "Add App Password"
  3. Name it "Kova Agent" and click "Create"
  4. Copy the generated password and paste it below

App passwords have limited access and can be revoked anytime. We never see your main password.
