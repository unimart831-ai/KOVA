How to read these numbers

Est. MRR = active subscribers × list price (not trialing). Trials = 7-day Starter limits (TRIAL_FEATURE_PLAN=starter), not Growth. Agency = KES 7,999 / $59, sales-only via is_agency_approved. M-Pesa Collected = actual subscription STK payments. Commerce checkout = buyer payments on public shop links (separate revenue stream). Compare MRR vs 30d cash to spot billing lag or churn.
