## Company

**KOVA** (Kova Agent) is a **Business Intelligence Operating System (BIOS)** for African small and medium enterprises. The platform replaces a digital marketing department — content strategy, creation, publishing, engagement, analytics, lead capture, email marketing, and strategic planning — with **six autonomous AI agents** operating 24/7.

- **Incorporation:** [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [JURISDICTION: Kenya] (registration in progress / [COMPANY REGISTRATION NUMBER])
- **Product status:** Live in production on Railway — not a prototype
- **Payments:** M-Pesa (Kenya) + Stripe (international)
- **Channels:** Nine social platforms + WhatsApp Business + REACH conversion tools

---

## Problem

There are **44 million MSMEs** in Africa. Social media is their primary storefront, yet fewer than **8%** maintain consistent presence.

| Barrier | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| Time | 3–5 hours/day for effective social management |
| Cost | Social managers cost KES 30,000–50,000/month; Western tools start at $25–249/month |
| Fragmentation | 4–6 tools for content, analytics, leads, and email |
| No conversion path | Followers rarely become measurable leads or sales |

Western tools solve scheduling or copywriting — not the **full intelligence loop** (research → create → publish → engage → capture → nurture → learn). None are built for **M-Pesa, KES pricing, and mobile-first Africa**.

---

## Solution

KOVA runs six AI agents in a continuous loop:

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| Create | Platform-native content from business ideas |
| Analyst | Content DNA, engagement prediction, learning |
| Research | Trend discovery and opportunity briefs |
| Adapt | Optimal scheduling from 30-day performance data |
| Engage | Sentiment, replies, superfans, auto-response |
| Chief Strategist | Orchestration, Daily Brief, proactive seeds |

**Conversion engine:** Kova Links (link-in-bio), lead capture forms, CRM-light pipeline, email marketing, Smart CTAs, A/B testing.

**Built and live:** 16 Django app modules, 12+ Celery background tasks, Growth Partner Program, multi-brand teams, content safety, platform resilience (3-strike recovery).
